## Header

No single form dominates. Attested openings:

```
ОТЗЫВ
иностранного научного консультанта на диссертационную работу
<ФИО в родительном падеже> на тему «<...>», представленную на соискание
степени доктора философии (PhD) по ОП: 8D061XX — «<название>»
```

```
REVIEW
of the scientific consultant for the dissertation work of <Full Name>
«<Title>», submitted for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy (PhD)
on educational program: 8D061XX — <name>
```

```
Review report for the PhD thesis, submitted by <Full Name>, from International
Information Technology University, Kazakhstan, for the PhD in 8D061XX — <name>.
```

Where the review is on institutional letterhead, the letterhead stands above this and the
consultant's contact details often appear in the footer.

## How the prose runs

The beats are the same as in the domestic consultant's review
([[../13-supervisor-review/structure]]) but weighted towards the science rather than towards the
candidate's development — a foreign consultant rarely comments on the studies themselves:

1. Relevance and socio-economic significance of the problem.
2. The scientific contribution — models, architectures, mechanisms — and how it differs from
   existing approaches.
3. Experimental evaluation: datasets, volumes, metrics, robustness, comparison with baselines.
4. Practical significance — scalability, cost, applicability, implementation potential.
5. The verdict.

**If sections are used** (the minority), those five make a defensible set — Олжаев's are, verbatim:
`1. Общий обзор`, `2. Научная новизна и методологический вклад`,
`3. Экспериментальная оценка и результаты`, `4. Практическая значимость`, `5. Заключение`.
Titled `##` headings without numbers are equally acceptable and are what our own review uses.

## Verdict formulas (verbatim from the samples)

**EN** — "… **deserves to be awarded** the degree of Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in the EP: 8D061XX."
or "… is **recommended for the awarding** of the Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) degree …"

**RU** — «… **заслуживает присуждения** указанной академической степени.»

The stronger EN closing seen in several samples pairs the two: the work "makes a significant,
original and scientifically substantiated contribution", "meets the academic and methodological
requirements for doctoral research and conforms to international standards for the award of a PhD",
and is therefore recommended.

## Signatory block

```
Professor,
<Department>,
<Faculty>,
<Organisation>,
<Postcode, City, Country>                                <Prof. Dr. Full Name>
```

No placeholder fields. Where the review is bilingual, **each half is signed separately**.
