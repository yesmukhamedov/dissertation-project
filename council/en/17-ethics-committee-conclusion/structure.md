## Who issues it

| | Samples | When |
|---|---|---|
| **IITU's own ethics commission** | 14 of 16 (13 in Russian, Ибраева in Kazakh) | the default |
| **An external accredited ethics committee**, on that body's letterhead, using the same 9-row form | 2 of 16 | Базарбеков — Local Ethics Committee of **Al-Farabi KazNU**, because the research involves patients; Тоқтарова — **Kh. A. Yasawi University**, her own employer |

The form does not change with the issuer — only the letterhead and the signatories do.

**This matters for us.** Our work uses fundus images and a Kazakhstani clinical set, which puts it
in Базарбеков's category — his topic (diagnosis of cognitive impairment) is the closest analogue in
the archive: same programme 8D06102, same department, defense 2026-05-29. Decide early whether the
conclusion comes from IITU's commission or from an accredited external committee, because an
external one takes its own submission cycle.

### Rows 4, 5 and 6 carry real content

Row 4 is not just the title — it also carries the **order number and approval date**
(`«<тема>» (приказ №5/1-Р от 20.12.2019 г.)`); Базарбеков's says `(переутверждение)` where the topic
was re-approved. Row 5 lists **both** consultants with degree, position, organisation and
citizenship. Row 6 is a 2–6 line description of what was actually studied, not a one-word answer.

### Rows 10–12 are new — the AI disclosure

⚠ The approved Appendix 4 form of the Model Statute still prints **9 rows**, but the text of the
Model Statute (rev. 23.06.2026) requires the conclusion to state the **absence or presence of facts
of AI use — on the basis of the candidate's own statement**.

Only **1 of 16** samples has these rows: **Базарбеков** (2026, Computer Engineering, 8D06102 — our
department and programme). His entries:

| Row | Entry |
|-----|-------|
| 10 | `Технологии ИИ использовались исключительно для языковой и стилистической корректуры текстов.` |
| 11 | `IRB-A1961` |
| 12 | `26.02.2026` |

The 15 pre-2026 samples stop at row 9. **Target the 12-row form.** Note the genre of row 10 in the
one sample we have: a narrow, concrete scope of use, no enumeration of tools.

Full norm on AI — [[../00-regulatory-framework/ai-usage]].

### Rows 7 and 8 are the only tick-box rows

Answer verbatim: `Нарушения не выявлены` / `Ешқандай бұзушылық табылмады`.

### Row 9 is prose, not a tick-box

The earlier version of this file offered "ensured / not ensured". No sample answers that way.

Where no living subjects are involved (the majority):

> В работе объекты живой природы и среды обитания не исследовались.
> Жұмыста тірі табиғат және тіршілік ету аймағы зерттелмеді.

Where there are human participants — Базарбеков, and the pattern we will need — the row is a
substantive paragraph naming the ethical framework and the safeguards. His, condensed:

> Защита прав, безопасности и благополучия участников исследования обеспечивалась согласно
> принципам Хельсинкской декларации ВМА и правилам надлежащей клинической практики. Исследование
> наблюдательное; [instrument] аппаратно безопасен; участие добровольное, на основании
> информированного согласия. Конфиденциальность: данные анонимизированы (ID) при регистрации,
> база не содержит персональных данных, информация используется в обобщённом виде.

The elements to cover: **the declared framework** (Helsinki Declaration, GCP), **study type**
(observational / interventional), **safety of any instrument**, **voluntary informed consent**, and
**anonymisation and confidentiality of the data**.
