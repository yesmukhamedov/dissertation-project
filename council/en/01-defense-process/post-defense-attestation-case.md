# After the defense: registration and the attestation case

> Section 5 of the Candidate's Guide ([[../00-regulatory-framework/candidate-guide]]).
> What the candidate does and what the academic secretary does once the council has voted.

## Deadlines

| Deadline | Who | What |
|----------|-----|------|
| **≤7 calendar days** after the defense | **the candidate** | registers the dissertation and the cover letter in the **NCSTE information system**. The cover letter is on University letterhead, signed by the chair of the council |
| **≤30 calendar days** after the defense | **the academic secretary** | compiles the **attestation case** and sends it to the Committee for Quality Assurance in Science and Higher Education of the MSHE RK |

## Contents of the attestation case (9 items)

1. **Cover letter / petition** on the letterhead of the university hosting the council, signed by
   the chair of the council, **stating the date the dissertation was sent to NCSTE**
   (except dissertations with state secrets / restricted use).
2. **The dissertation on electronic media.** With state secrets / restricted use — also on paper.
3. **List and copies of scientific publications** on the dissertation topic.
4. **Copy of the identity document.**
5. **NCSTE certificate** on the plagiarism check **and the check for uncited use of artificial
   intelligence technologies**. For dissertations with state secrets / restricted use — the
   certificate of the corresponding commission in military and special educational institutions
   and/or research organizations subordinate to the national security bodies, the Ministry of
   Internal Affairs, the prosecution bodies and the Ministry of Defense of the RK.
6. **Attendance sheet** of the council members ([[../20-attendance-sheet/structure]]).
7. **Full video recording** and the **minutes** of the council session on the defense — and on the
   discussion of the revised dissertation (if applicable); the minutes are signed by the chair of
   the council and the academic secretary.
8. **Copy of the transcript** of the doctoral programme.
9. **Information on the doctoral candidate** per the form in Appendix 3 of the Guide
   ([[../19-doctoral-candidate-info/structure]]).

## Submission format

Items **1), 3), 4), 5), 6), 8) and 9)** are submitted to the Committee **as scans, in PDF**
(except attestation cases containing state secrets / restricted-use information).

So only **2)** the dissertation on electronic media and **7)** the video recording with the
minutes go in a non-PDF form.
