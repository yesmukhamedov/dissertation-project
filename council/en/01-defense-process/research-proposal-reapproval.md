# Re-approval of the Research Proposal

> Section 2 of the Candidate's Guide ([[../00-regulatory-framework/candidate-guide]]).
> **Who it applies to:** doctoral candidates who completed the doctoral programme coursework
> **more than 3 (three) years ago** and have not defended — after being reinstated as IITU
> doctoral candidates.
>
> This is **stage 0** of the defense route: without a re-approved topic, admission to defense
> cannot be processed.

## Procedure

1. **Within 1 (one) month of reinstatement** the candidate submits the Research Proposal to
   the department.
2. The department meeting considers:
   - relevance of the research topic;
   - the extent to which the research content matches current directions of science development;
   - whether to keep or change the topic.
3. Fork:
   - **topic found relevant** → the department decides to keep the previously approved topic;
     an **extract from the department meeting minutes** is issued and submitted to the Office
     of Postgraduate Education;
   - **correction needed** to the Research Proposal and/or the topic → the candidate submits an
     **updated** Research Proposal to a department meeting; the department then recommends
     re-approval of the proposal and/or the topic; **the minutes extract + the Research Proposal**
     go to the Office.
4. The Director of the Office puts the matter before the **University Academic Council**.
5. On the basis of the Academic Council decision, an **order on re-approval** of the Research
   Proposal and/or the topic is issued.

## Outputs

- Extract from the department meeting minutes.
- (if changed) Updated Research Proposal.
- **Order on re-approval** — the basis for proceeding with admission to defense. An extract from
  this order later belongs to the document set submitted to the council
  (see [[application-to-council]], item 7).
