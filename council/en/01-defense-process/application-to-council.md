# Application to the dissertation council

> Section 4 of the Candidate's Guide ([[../00-regulatory-framework/candidate-guide]]).
> The stage between a positive conclusion of the extended department meeting
> ([[preliminary-review]]) and publication of the materials online ([[materials-published-online]]).

## 1. Document set for the council (10 items)

1. **Application** by the candidate addressed to the Chairman of the Board – Rector, requesting
   admission to defense — **written by hand** per the sample in Appendix 2 of the Guide.
   Approval signatures: scientific consultant, chair of the dissertation council, Director of the
   Office of Postgraduate Education (room 609).
2. **Originals of the reviews** by the domestic and foreign scientific consultants.
3. **Positive conclusion of the extended department meeting.**
4. **The dissertation.** ⚠ It must contain **references to the candidate's own publications**
   (required by paras. 5-1 and 6 of the Rules for Awarding Degrees).
5. **Annotation in three languages** (Kazakh, English, Russian), recommended length —
   **no more than 5 pages** in each language.
6. **List of scientific works** (Form 2.3) **and copies of the works**.
7. **Extracts from orders**: approving the dissertation topic and the scientific consultants;
   admitting the candidate to defense.
8. **Transcript.**
9. **Ethics committee conclusion.**
10. Candidates **from other universities** — a cover letter on the university letterhead
    addressed to the Chairman of the Board – Rector of IITU.

## 2. Application form (Appendix 2 of the Guide)

Handwritten, addressed to the Chairman of the Board – Rector of IITU, from the doctoral
candidate under educational programme "___".

Required content blocks:

- "I request that my dissertation on the topic «___», submitted for the degree of Doctor of
  Philosophy (PhD) under educational programme «___» (code and title of the programme), be
  accepted for consideration and subsequent defense."
- Scientific consultants (degree, academic title, full name).
- "The dissertation was carried out at the department «___», «___» (department) (university)."
- "The dissertation is submitted for defense **for the first time**" / if repeated — state the
  date of the first defense.
- **Consent to personal data processing**: inclusion in the attestation case, further processing,
  and publication on the University web resource.
- **Authenticity statement**: "all data and results submitted for defense are genuine and were
  obtained by me personally."
- Date, signature.
- Approvals (signatures): Director of the Office of Postgraduate Education · Head of Department ·
  Scientific consultant.

## 3. Intake and scheduling deadlines

| Deadline | What happens |
|----------|--------------|
| ≤2 working days | the academic secretary accepts and **registers** the documents and submits them to the council |
| ≤10 working days from intake | the council **sets the defense date** and appoints **two reviewers** |
| ≤3 months | between the scheduling decision and the defense date |

Defense dates are set **in order of submission** of candidates' documents.

## 4. Materials for the defense announcement (to the Office)

Once the defense date, the temporary council members and the official reviewers are appointed,
the candidate submits to the Office, for publication on the University official web resources:

1. **Announcement in three languages** in Word format — **3 separate files**.
2. **Dissertation in PDF** — **copy-protected**. Example file name: `Диссертация Байжанов ГС`.
3. **Annotation** in Kazakh, Russian and English — **PDF, 3 separate files**, with a combined
   length of **no less than 1 printed sheet**.
4. **List of scientific works** — in **`doc` and `pdf`** (the pdf being a scanned copy of the
   certified document).
5. **Scientific consultants' reviews** in **pdf**. If the foreign consultant review is in a
   language other than the language of defense, a scanned copy of the **notarized translation**
   is also required. ⚠ The notary personal data must be **anonymized (hidden)**, except for
   what is needed to confirm the fact of notarization.

The announcement is also posted on the web resource of the **Committee for Quality Assurance in
Science and Higher Education** within **5 working days** of reaching the Committee (except
materials containing state secrets or restricted-use information).

Full list of published materials — [[materials-published-online]].

## 5. Plagiarism and AI-usage check

⚠ **New ground for the check:** alongside plagiarism, the dissertation is checked for
**use of artificial intelligence technologies without citing them**.

| Deadline | Action |
|----------|--------|
| ≤10 working days after admission to defense | the council sends the dissertation to **NCSTE** for a check against domestic and international databases — for plagiarism **and for uncited AI use**. **The title page and the list of references are not checked** |
| — | a **commission of the dissertation council** is formed from council members to check for borrowed material without attribution |
| ≥8 working days before the defense | the commission submits its **conclusion** to the council — triggered where facts of plagiarism and/or uncited AI use appear in the NCSTE certificate, in the official reviewers' reports, or in informal feedback on the University web resource |
| ≥7 working days before the defense | on the basis of the commission conclusion the council **admits the candidate to defense** or **withdraws the dissertation** |
| ≤2 working days after the decision | the council notifies the candidate; the information is posted on the University web resource |
| ≥7 working days before the defense | the **candidate may withdraw** the dissertation themselves |
