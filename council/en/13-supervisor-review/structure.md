## Header

The dominant Russian form (9 of 16) — the candidate's name in the genitive, the genre word first:

```
ОТЗЫВ
научного консультанта на диссертационную работу
<Фамилия Имя Отчество в родительном падеже>
на тему «<...>», представленную на соискание степени доктора
философии (PhD) по ОП: 8D061XX — «<название программы>»
```

Attested variants: `ОТЗЫВ отечественного научного консультанта…` (used when a foreign consultant
also signs a separate review — our case), `ОТЗЫВ НАУЧНОГО РУКОВОДИТЕЛЯ…`, and a bare
`ОТЗЫВ на диссертационную работу…`.

**In Kazakh the order inverts** — the description comes first and the genre word `ПІКІРІ` stands
alone at the end, in the possessive form:

```
<8D061XX — «<бағдарлама>»> білім беру бағдарламасы бойынша
философия докторы (PhD) дәрежесін алу үшін ұсынылған
ізденуші <Фамилия Имя Отчество, ілік септік>
«<тақырып>» тақырыбындағы
диссертациялық жұмысына ғылыми кеңесшінің

                    ПІКІРІ
```

Not `# ПІКІР` at the top: the nominative bare form is not attested.

## How the prose runs

Unlabelled paragraphs in roughly this order. This is a description of what the samples do, **not a
set of headings to insert** — the beats must stay invisible.

1. What the dissertation is devoted to, or the aim of the research, in one sentence.
2. The problem domain and why it matters — social, clinical or economic framing.
3. Why the chosen approach is the promising one for that problem.
4. What the candidate actually built: models, architectures, algorithms, datasets, instruments.
5. That the conclusions follow from the work and are confirmed experimentally.
6. Scientific novelty, and that the results were **obtained by the candidate independently**.
7. Practical significance and where the results can be applied.
8. The candidate as a researcher during the studies — independence, scientific maturity — and the
   publications: counts by tier (WoS/Scopus, KKSON, conferences), conference presentations.
   See [[../00-regulatory-framework/publication-requirements]].
9. The verdict.

## Verdict formulas (verbatim from the samples)

**RU** — «Диссертационная работа … соответствует требованиям, предъявляемым к диссертациям по
данному научному направлению, а её автор *<ФИО>* **заслуживает присуждения степени доктора
философии (PhD)** по образовательной программе 8D061XX — «<название>».»

**KZ** — «… философия докторы (PhD) дәрежесіне ие болуға **лайықты деп есептеймін**.» or
«… философия докторы (PhD) дәрежесін алу талаптарын **қанағаттандырады деп есептеймін**.»
Both attested; both put the judgement in the first person.

**EN** — "… **deserves to be awarded** the academic degree of Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in the
EP: 8D061XX."

## Signatory block

```
Ғылыми кеңесші:                          ← or `Научный консультант:` / `Scientific consultant:`
<degree, academic title>,
<position, department>,
<organisation>                                          <Surname A.B.>
```

Left column carries the credentials, the name sits flush right, the wet signature goes between them.
Do not add `Signature:`, `Date:` or `Stamp:` fields — no sample has any.

## Ours

`thesis/output/supervisor_review_kz.md` → `defense/docs/reviews/supervisor_review_kz.{docx,pdf}`.
Kazakh, because the defense is in Kazakh (see `PROJECT_MEMORY/defense-language-kazakh.md`) and the
consultant is domestic; the foreign consultant signs separately in English
([[../14-foreign-consultant-review/structure]]). Our text is prose without headings and ends on the
attested verdict formula, which matches the genre.
