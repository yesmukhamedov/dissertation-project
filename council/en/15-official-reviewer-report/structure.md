# Official reviewer's report (Appendix 3) — norms, genre and format

> **Rewritten against the real submissions** in `D:/dissertation_council/Образцы документов/авторы/`
> (Бакирова, Әйтім, Дауренбаева, Тоқтарова, Мырзакерімова) and against §6 of the
> [[../00-regulatory-framework/council-statute|Statute]] (R-39, rev. 5, pp. 9–12).
> The blank form itself is in [[form-blank]].
> Unlike the supervisor / foreign-consultant reviews — where the templates were wrong in genre and the
> samples had to be trusted instead — **here the strict tabular form is real**: every sample follows it.

## Binding rules (Statute §6)

| § | Rule |
|---|------|
| 6.6 | Official reviewers submit **written reports on the form of Appendix 3**, based on studying the dissertation *and the published works*. For a series-of-articles defense they also comment on the scientific level of each article; if **≥ 2/3** of the articles are unrelated to the topic, the reviewer does not consider the work. |
| 6.7 | The report must state **exactly one** of three decisions: (1) petition the Committee to award the PhD / Doctor by profile; (2) send the dissertation back for revision (not available for series-of-articles defenses); (3) refuse the award. |
| 6.8 | **Copies of the reports are handed to the candidate no later than 5 working days before the defense.** A replacement reviewer decided later than that moves the defense date. |
| 6.9 (6) | The reports are **published on the university internet resource 5 working days before the set defense date** and stay available at least 5 months after it. |
| 6.6 excl. | Cannot be an official reviewer: Committee staff; **co-authors** of the candidate's publications on the topic; heads of the structural units / organisations where the work was done or that commissioned it; anyone who was a scientific consultant, or who gave a positive report on a dissertation the Committee then rejected within the last 3 years; close relatives. |
| §7.7 (6) | At the session the reports are **read out** by the reviewers, and the candidate then answers their comments (item 7). See [[../01-defense-process/session-procedure]]. |

**Two** official reviewers are appointed (the samples always contain exactly two files:
`Отзыв_официального_рецензента_1` / `_2`, or `Reviewer1` / `Reviewer2`).

## What the real submissions look like

| | Real samples | What the earlier template here said |
|---|---|---|
| Form | **Confirmed** — 13-criterion table, 3 columns, exactly as Appendix 3 | same (correct) |
| Orientation | **Landscape.** Verified 2026-08-13 across all six readable samples: Әйтім ×2 are born-digital A4 landscape (297×210); Базарбекова #2 carries `/Rotate 270`; Бакирова, Тоқтарова and Мырзакерімова are landscape sheets scanned onto portrait pages, so their content runs sideways. Not one readable sample is typeset portrait — the four columns do not fit otherwise. | not stated |
| Sub-criteria | **Each numbered sub-criterion gets its own table row** — 4.1…4.5, 7.1…7.5, 8.1…8.5, 9.1…9.3 — with its own justification cell opposite it; the criterion name cell spans them. Options inside a cell are stacked **one per line**, not run together. | not stated |
| Length | **9–26 pages**, most 12–19 (median 13.5 over the 12 samples) | not stated |
| Language | Follows the **reviewer**, not the defense: Бакирова RU, Дауренбаева EN, Тоқтарова KZ, Әйтім **EN + RU, both halves in one file, each signed** | not stated |
| Column 3 | Real substantive prose, 3–15 lines per row, naming the candidate's actual artefacts, metrics, journals and quartiles | "comments — in italics" only |
| Selection | The chosen option is **underlined** inside column 2; unchosen options stay visible | same (correct) |
| Criterion 11 | The **only place where criticism appears** — 1–3 concrete, verifiable defects (a table that repeats another, an unclear sample size, typography/spelling). Written in *italics*. Reviewer 1 and reviewer 2 raise **different** defects. | "list of critical comments" |
| Criterion 13 | One line only: *"To petition the Committee for the award of the degree of Doctor of Philosophy (PhD)"* | same (correct) |
| After the table | A short **`Conclusion` / `Заключение`** paragraph — restates candidate, topic, that the work is a completed independent piece of research meeting the "Rules for awarding degrees", and that its author deserves the petition | absent |
| Signatory | `Official reviewer:` then degree/title, department, organisation/city down the **left**, full name flush **right**, wet signature + organisation's HR stamp between them. **No `Signature: ___` / `Date: "__"` placeholder fields.** | placeholder fields — wrong |
| Footer | A stamped note may appear: *"Copies of reviews from official reviewers are given to the doctoral student no later than 5 (five) working days before the defense."* | absent |

⚠ The `Signature:/Date:/Stamp:` placeholder block is a template-ism that **no** real submission carries —
the same defect already corrected in [[../13-supervisor-review/structure]] and
[[../14-foreign-consultant-review/structure]]. Leave the signature area as plain lines for a wet signature.

## Header (verbatim shape)

English (Дауренбаева, Әйтім):

```
REVIEW OF THE OFFICIAL REVIEWER
on the dissertation work of <Surname Name Patronymic>
on the topic «<Title>»,
provided for the degree of Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in the educational program: 8D06104 — Computer systems and software engineering
```

Russian (Бакирова, Әйтім RU half):

```
ОТЗЫВ ОФИЦИАЛЬНОГО РЕЦЕНЗЕНТА
на диссертационную работу <Фамилия Имя Отчество, род. п.>
на тему «<Название>»,
представленную на соискание степени доктора философии (PhD)
по образовательной программе 8D06104 — «Вычислительная техника и программное обеспечение»
```

## Table columns

| № | Criteria / Критерии | Eligibility (please select one answer option) / Соответствие критериям (подчеркнуть один из вариантов ответа) | Justification of the official reviewer's position / Обоснование позиции официального рецензента (замечания выделить курсивом) |

Column 2 carries the **fixed** option lists of the form; column 3 is what the reviewer writes.
