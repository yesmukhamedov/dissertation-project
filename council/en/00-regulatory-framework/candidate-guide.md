# PhD Candidate's Guide (GD-01, IITU «МУИТ»)

> **Source:** `GD_01_Путеводитель_соискателя_степени_PhD_русс.docx` — an internal normative
> document of IITU, binding on doctoral candidates, faculty and everyone involved in
> preparing and holding a defense.
> **Status:** enters into force upon approval; amendments are approved by a decision of the IITU Board.
>
> The Guide is a **procedural** document: it does not replace the Statute on the council R-39
> ([[council-statute]]) but fixes the concrete deadlines, document sets and internal approval
> route. Where details differ, the Guide prevails as the later internal norm.

## Cross-cutting deadlines set by the Guide

| Deadline | Event |
|----------|-------|
| 1 month after reinstatement | submit the Research Proposal to the department (for those who finished coursework >3 years ago) |
| ≥1 month before the extended meeting | the chair appoints ≥2 department reviewers |
| ≤2 working days | the academic secretary registers the documents submitted to the council |
| ≤10 working days | the council sets the defense date and appoints two official reviewers |
| ≤10 working days after admission to defense | the dissertation goes to NCSTE for a plagiarism check **and a check for uncited use of AI** |
| ≤3 months | between the decision scheduling the defense and the defense itself |
| ≥8 working days before the defense | conclusion of the council's plagiarism commission |
| ≥7 working days before the defense | council decision to admit / withdraw; also the last date the candidate may withdraw the dissertation |
| ≤2 working days | the council notifies the candidate of the decision |
| ≤5 working days | the announcement is posted on the Committee's web resource |
| ≤7 calendar days after the defense | the candidate registers the dissertation in the NCSTE information system |
| ≤30 calendar days after the defense | the academic secretary sends the attestation case to the Committee |
