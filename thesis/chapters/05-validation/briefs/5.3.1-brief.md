### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Section objective:** Place the dissertation's results in the published landscape, and establish structurally — through the table's own columns — that no ranking against a named system is available from the evidence.

**Argumentative spine:**
- **Thesis:** The published systems and this dissertation measure different quantities, on different corpora, against different reference standards; the comparison is therefore contextual, it cannot be resolved into an ordering, and the incommensurability is a property of the evidence rather than a hedge adopted for caution.
- **Chain:**
  1. State the constraint before the table: no controlled experiment against any named system was run, none of the published systems is reproducible from its report at the level required, and the dissertation's endpoints differ from theirs.
  2. **Table 5.5 (TAB-5.3)** — one row per system with columns that *carry* the incommensurability: task definition, corpus and partition, reference standard, metric, reported value. The dissertation's own row appears last and is marked as measuring a different quantity.
  3. **Read the table along the axis of difference, not of value.** Four axes: task (binary referable versus five-class ordinal), reference standard (adjudicated specialist panels versus public dataset labels), corpus and partition (private prospective cohorts versus public retrospective benchmarks), and metric (operating-point sensitivity/specificity versus weighted F1, κ, ROC-AUC).
  4. **The reference-standard axis deserves separate treatment** — a system validated against an adjudicated panel is being measured against a stronger label than one validated against single-grader public labels, and this alone can move reported figures in either direction.
  5. **Where a comparison is legitimate**: the dissertation's referable-DR operating characteristics can be *reported alongside* published operating points as context on the same nominal endpoint, provided the corpus and reference-standard differences are stated in the same breath. Do this once, carefully, and draw no ordering.
  6. **De Fauw et al. (2018)** as the instructive non-comparable: an OCT-based referral system, different modality entirely — included to show the range of what "DR screening system" covers in the literature.
  7. **The P1 observation that is permitted:** several of these reports do not describe their preprocessing in the main text. State this as observable methodological practice — it is the empirical basis of the P1 characterization and of the reproducibility limit — using the permitted phrasings only.
  8. Close: what the comparison licenses (contextual placement; the observation that the dissertation's figures are of the same order as published work on nominally similar endpoints) and what it does not (any claim of superiority, equivalence, or ranking).

**Required tables:** Table 5.5 (TAB-5.3), assembled from the literature cards.

### COUNTER-ARGUMENT & INHERITED LIMITATIONS
