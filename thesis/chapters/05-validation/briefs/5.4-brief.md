### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Section objective:** State, in one place and without mitigation, every boundary on the work's claims, organized by kind rather than by code, and identify the one limitation that rises to a methodological contribution.

**Argumentative spine:**
- **Thesis:** The work's claims are bounded on five axes — the structure of the evidence, the measurements not made, generalization, interpretation, and reproducibility — and one recurring defect in the evaluation measures used by this literature is severe enough to be reported as a finding rather than as a concession.
- **Chain:**
  1. **Register statement first:** a limitation is a boundary on a claim, not a confession; every item is attributed to the section that established it, and none is re-argued.
  2. **The centrepiece — the normalization defect.** Three instances of one structural fault: the generalization ratio (§4.5.1), the retired degradation form (§4.7), the retention ratio (§4.8). Each normalizes or differences external performance against an arm's **own** in-domain performance, and therefore **penalizes in-domain strength**. Give the identity that makes it exact: Δ_drop(D) − Δ_drop(C) ≡ Δ_in-domain − Δ_external. State the consequence for how such measures should be read, and present it as a **secondary methodological contribution stated descriptively** — never as a defence of any particular result of this work.
  3. **Limits in the structure of the evidence:** single-fold evaluation outside Experiment 1 with the resulting interval understatement (§5.2.1); multiplicity correction bounded to one experiment; small samples (the mask subset, the soft-exudate subset, the clinical hold-out); a single annotated corpus for the attention evidence.
  4. **Measurements not made:** clinical attention overlays (G-3); cross-corpus attention consistency; the mask-channel contribution not isolated; the photometric parameter interaction unswept; per-grouping confusion matrices unrecorded; three domain-distance protocol parameters unrecoverable; the preprocessing stage's own computational cost unmeasured.
  5. **Generalization boundaries:** DGL-1, DGL-3, DGL-5 with NC-10, NC-11, NC-12, NC-17, DGL-2, SB-1.1, SB-3.1, SB-3.2, SB-1.7.
  6. **Interpretive boundaries:** NC-14 with SB-1.11; SB-1.10 on calibration; NC-16 with SB-1.8; SB-1.3; NC-15; SB-1.2; CFC-2.4 / NC-3.
  7. **Reproducibility and comparison boundaries:** SB-2.2; SB-2.1 and SB-2.3; SB-2.4 and DGL-6 with the CFC-2.8 composite in its current decomposable form; NC-13 and SIR-5 on self-citation independence; SB-1.12 on the paradigm representative; the §5.3.1 incommensurability limit.
  8. **The design-only chapter:** SB-4.1, SB-4.2, SB-4.3, DGL-4, and SB-1.6 with SIR-8 on projected outcomes.
  9. **Close with what survives:** the controlled comparison under identical conditions, its consistency across every condition examined, and its decomposition — stated at established strength, not restated upward.
