### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Section objective:** Set out which source of uncertainty each statistical procedure addresses, which experiment supports which procedure, and where the resulting inference is weaker than the reported intervals suggest.

**Argumentative spine:**
- **Thesis:** The dissertation's statistical claims rest on three distinct and non-interchangeable sources of variability; only Experiment 1 supports all three, the external evaluations quantify evaluation-set sampling alone and therefore **understate total uncertainty in a known direction**, and the correction for multiplicity is bounded to one experiment rather than to the evidence base as a whole.
- **Chain:**
  1. Distinguish the three sources: (a) training variability across folds, (b) evaluation-set sampling at fixed models, (c) paired case-level discordance. State that conflating them is the failure mode this section exists to prevent.
  2. Table 5.2 — inference procedure by experiment. Read it: only Experiment 1 admits all three.
  3. Table 5.3 (TAB-5.1) — the Experiment 1 suite. **Calibrated language mandatory.** Note that non-overlap of marginal bootstrap intervals is *weaker* than the paired tests; the paired tests are primary.
  4. **The mixed-effects model stated precisely:** fold as random effect, arm and backbone as fixed; interaction p = 0.31. A non-rejection is **absence of evidence for heterogeneity, not evidence of homogeneity**, and the test has limited power at two backbones. EH-4 is met as specified — this refines the statement, it does not withdraw it.
  5. **Multiplicity, honestly scoped:** Holm was applied within Experiment 1 across four configurations. **No family-wise correction spans the seven hypotheses**, which were pre-specified with distinct criteria on distinct data and are not a family of exchangeable tests — but no dissertation-wide error rate is claimed either.
  6. **The single-fold consequence:** for Experiments 3–6 the intervals are per-instance and quantify sampling of the evaluation corpus only. Total uncertainty is understated; the *direction* of the bias is known even though its size is not. Correct reading: differences established under the fitted models, not estimates of expected difference over retraining.
  7. Two residual items: the Experiment 2 dispersion heuristic named as a heuristic; three domain-distance protocol parameters unrecoverable (§4.4.1), which bounds independent reproduction of those intervals.
  8. Hand-off: §5.2.2 assigns strengths under exactly these conditions.
