**Key concepts established:**
- **Table 5.6** — measured cost of all four configurations at 512² single-precision inference on the documented hardware, with measurement conditions in the caption.
- **The cheap-prior result, in quantitative form:** the fourth input channel costs ≈ +0.9 % of operations, ≈ 24 MiB of inference memory, a stem-level parameter increase invisible at the reported precision, and latency within measurement spread — against the in-domain gain of §4.2.3. *"as far as the network is concerned, the integrated configuration is a very cheap prior… representation is not something the network has to pay for."*
- **The confinement stated in the same passage, not deferred:** the eight preprocessing stages run on the processor and **were not benchmarked**; Table 5.6 *"describes the cheaper half of the system."* What would close the gap is specified (timing the stages under the same discipline, with the caching arrangement made explicit).
- **Correction one — operations do not predict time.** ≈ 4.3× fewer operations and ≈ 2.2× fewer parameters yield ≈ 9 % at batch 16 and a **reversal at batch 1** (12.8–14.5 ms against 10.5). Consequence made procedural: *"any performance–complexity trade-off stated in this work is stated from measured wall-clock time, not from operation counts."* SIR-7 and NC-6 stated at that point.
- **Correction two — parameters do not predict memory.** Training footprint ≈ 13.7 GiB against ≈ 3.7 GiB, in the opposite order to parameter count; activation memory at 512² in single precision dominates. **This explains the protocol's batch size of sixteen** (§4.1.3): the limit follows from activation size, not model size. The paging condition of the larger measurement is disclosed.
- **The licence handed to Chapter 6:** the integrated configuration is adoptable at essentially no inference-side cost on either architecture — **a parameter for the design argument, not a demonstration of deployability** (SB-4.1).
- **SIR-4 deliberately not invoked:** the prior self-published accuracy-to-computation observation is *not* cited here, because these measurements bear on it directly and SIR-7 forbids the class-level generalization it would invite. Recorded as deliberate.
