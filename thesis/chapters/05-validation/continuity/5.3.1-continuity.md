- **The evasion objection answered:** *"the refusal is forced rather than chosen: to rank these systems one would need a common task, a common corpus, a common reference standard and a common metric, and no two rows of Table 5.5 share all four."* What can be said instead: the results are of the expected order, and the distinctive evidence is **the controlled comparison between two configurations under identical conditions, which none of the cited reports provides.**

**Argument thread:** External placement is complete and yields context, not rank. The comparison now turns inward — from what other systems achieve to what this work's own configurations cost — where measurement is possible because it is made on the dissertation's own models.

**Final topic:** The P1 practice observation and its practical consequence for controlled comparison, with the pointer to §5.3.2.

**Setup for §5.3.2 (Performance–Complexity Trade-Off Analysis):**
- ✅ Ready — measured parameters, GFLOPs, latency at batch 1 and 16, throughput, and inference/training memory for all four configurations, on the documented hardware at 512² input.
- **The section's central quantitative finding:** the fourth input channel costs ≈ +0.9 % GFLOPs, ≈ +24 MiB inference memory, and latency within measurement noise, against the in-domain gain established in §4.2.3 — *the pipeline is a cheap prior at the network, and its cost is borne in preprocessing*. This is the quantitative form of the central thesis and should be stated as such.
- **The counter-intuitive result that must not be smoothed:** EfficientNet-B3 is ≈ 4.3× cheaper in FLOPs and ≈ 2.2× lighter in parameters than ResNet-50, yet only ≈ 9 % faster at batch 16 and **slower at batch 1**. **The trade-off argument must be made from measured wall-clock time, not from FLOPs** — depthwise convolution utilizes the hardware differently.
- **The memory finding:** training memory for EfficientNet-B3 at 512² in full precision exceeded the device's physical memory and was serviced by host-memory paging. The batch-size limit follows from activation size at 512², **not** from parameter count. State it as a property of the measurement conditions.
- **DGL-2 binds every efficiency statement** to the documented hardware; **SIR-7** forbids generalizing from one efficient architecture to the class of efficient architectures.
- **No cross-system efficiency comparison** — published systems do not report comparable cost measurements, and CFC-2.2 / NC-2 continue to bind.
- The preprocessing cost itself is a CPU cost and was **not** benchmarked; say so rather than implying the pipeline is free.
