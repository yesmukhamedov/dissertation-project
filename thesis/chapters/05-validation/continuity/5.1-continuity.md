**Key concepts established:**
- **Chapter 5's function stated at the outset** — the same body of evidence, four new questions: reliability, claim strength, relation to published work, boundaries. §5.1 takes the explainability channel first *because it is the only channel that measures something other than predictive performance*.
- **The corroboration argument (the section's substantive contribution, dissertation-original).** A classification gain admits two accounts — better use of pathological evidence, or better exploitation of corpus-specific regularities outside lesion regions. Both predict the §4.2.3 metrics; only the first predicts attention movement *toward* annotated lesions. The attention evidence therefore *"bears on the interpretation of the classification gain rather than on its magnitude"*, by *"making one class of explanations less comfortable"*. Explicitly labelled an interpretation, not a demonstration.
- **Three limits imposed in the same passage, at equal weight:** (i) the channels were measured on **different models** — separate backbone, single fold, own training configuration — so corroboration is **directional, not quantitative**; (ii) NC-14 — alignment is not localization; (iii) one annotated corpus, 54 images, soft exudates on 26, one centre, one camera.
- **The magnitude non-comparison made an affirmative rule**, not a caveat: *"those two margins are not commensurable… No comparison between them is made anywhere in this dissertation, and the smaller margin is not evidence of a smaller effect."* No numeral from §4.2.3 stands beside a numeral from §4.6 anywhere in the section.
- **NC-14 recast as a strength ceiling** rather than a closing disclaimer — *"the claim of attention alignment may be carried at the strength its statistics support, and no further in kind, however strong those statistics are."* This is the form in which PC-7 passes to §5.2.2.
- **FIG-5.1 declared an illustration carrying no measurement**; gallery routed to Appendix E; *"no claim in this dissertation rests on visual inspection of an overlay."*
- **Two absences recorded in §5.1 rather than deferred:** cross-corpus attention consistency (never measured; not inferable from transfer — §4.6.3), and the clinical qualitative overlays (G-3). Both stated as absences, not negative results; both carried to §5.4.
