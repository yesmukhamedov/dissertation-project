- **The four levels defined** (STRONG / MODERATE / CONDITIONAL / REFUTED) plus DESIGN/THEORETICAL for non-empirical claims. **Every empirical claim entered the programme at CONDITIONAL.**
- **Table 5.4 (TAB-5.2)** — ten primary claims with pre-specified condition, outcome, level and the qualification carried. (PC-3 is not in use in the register; stated once, not dwelt on.)
- **Outcome: seven empirical claims STRONG, none MODERATE, none REFUTED; PC-0/PC-4/PC-5 DESIGN/THEORETICAL.**
- **The inflation objection engaged in the body, answered in three parts:** (i) conditions fixed in advance and checkable; (ii) the classification is of the claim **as stated**, and the claims are stated narrowly — *"the discriminating work in this dissertation was done at specification time, in the fences… not at scoring time"*, with the honest corollary that a narrow confirmed claim *"says less than a broader one would"*; (iii) the criteria could have failed and not everything was promoted.
- **The failable criterion exhibited: PC-8.** Promotion turned on stage contributions separating by ≈ 3× the fold-level dispersion; comparable magnitudes — *"entirely consistent with the design"* — would have left it below promotion as an ensemble of indistinguishable stages.
- **Two observations deliberately not promoted:** the regularization reading and the calibration improvement, both MODERATE — no pre-specified acceptance threshold, each from a single experiment. **PC-0 gains nothing** from any empirical result (CFC-2.1).
- **Four travelling qualifications, inseparable from the labels:** PC-7 within NC-14, one corpus, one fold, qualitative half unevaluated; PC-8 grouping resolution (NC-17); **PC-10's 0.0041 margin with its lower bound +0.0362 lying below the MCID — disclosed as the writer's job, not the reader's**; PC-6/PC-9 thresholds cleared by both arms, so the evidence is in the comparison and in dispersion reduction.
- **CFC-2.8 restated in its current form:** the composite became **decomposable** (ablation under one initialization reproduces the whole in-domain gain) but is **not dissolved**; PC-1's strength attaches to the integrated configuration.
- **The meaning of STRONG bounded before the close:** confirmed as stated, on the corpora examined, under §5.2.1's conditions including the interval understatement — *"It does not mean established in general, and it carries no implication of clinical validity or readiness for use."*
