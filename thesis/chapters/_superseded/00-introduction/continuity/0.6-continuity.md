**Key concepts established:**
- **The central hypothesis is a two-link causal claim** — a change in the distribution of the images, and a
  consequence of that change for classification — and the seven hypotheses are decompositions of it. **H-3
  tests the first link, the other six the second.** This framing is what turns the register from a list into
  an argument, and every later restatement of the hypotheses should preserve it.
- **H-1's criterion is conjunctive and the exclusion is stated**: weighted F1 ≥ 5 pp *and* ROC-AUC ≥ 0.02
  *and* no κ degradation, simultaneously — *"Improvement on a subset of the three does not constitute
  dominance."* EH-4's external-direction and cross-architecture replication requirements are named with it.
- **CFC-2.8 is inside the H-1 statement**, as a property of the design: the independent variable is
  composite, the assertion is about the *configuration* as a unitary system, and attribution to
  preprocessing alone, initialisation alone, or their interaction *"is not claimable under H-1 and is not
  claimed anywhere in this dissertation."*
- **H-3 carries all three of its mandatory disclosures** — the label-reuse notice (the retired
  training-method hypothesis stands retired; "H-3 dropped" in §2.3.2/§3.3.3 refers to that one); the
  source-domain-statistics protocol condition with its consequence (otherwise the measurement is domain
  adaptation and incomparable with H-4/H-6/H-7); and the threshold provenance (MCID_d and K assigned at
  formalisation, not pre-registered, bounded by the unnormalised-units argument and by insensitivity to K).
- **H-7 is correct on its four precision points**: per-corpus, non-aggregated with the reversal rule,
  CI⁻ > 0 and *not* CI⁻ ≥ MCID, and **performance not resistance** — with the retired Δ_drop form named as
  retired and routed to Chapter 5 as a critique of a metric in common use.
- **NC-14 sits in H-5 and NC-16 in H-6**, in the hypothesis statements themselves.
- **VCR-3 stated in the close**: a contradicting result is reported as a falsifying observation, never
  absorbed by a silent restatement.
- **No outcome anywhere.** All numerals in the section are thresholds. This is the property on which
  §5.2.2's entire argument depends and it must not be eroded by any later edit to this section.

**Terms introduced:** Attention–Lesion Overlap and generalisation ratio G appear with their defining roles;
Δd, Δ wF1, CI⁻ and MCID enter as notation. All are formalised in Chapters 2 and 3.
