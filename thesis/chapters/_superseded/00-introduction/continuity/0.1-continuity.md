# CONTINUITY NOTE — §0.1 Relevance of the Research

**Version:** 7.1.0 · First argued section of the manuscript. Hands off to §0.3 (Research Goal).

---

**Key concepts established:**
- **The inversion that defines the problem.** Because early DR is asymptomatic, detection cannot depend on
  presentation but on scheduled imaging of a cohort defined by a systemic diagnosis. Screening is therefore
  *"before it is a diagnostic problem, a problem of volume"* — the sentence the rest of the argument turns on.
- **The capacity constraint stated arithmetically, not rhetorically.** Fixed and concentrated specialist
  supply against a large dispersed population; expanding the workforce is not a near-term option; therefore
  the grading step is the one that must be partly automated. Kazakhstan figures (≈1,200 ophthalmologists,
  ≈40% rural) cited as prior own work and as context only.
- **The projections disowned in the sentence that introduces them.** Access, complication-reduction and
  cost-reduction figures are named, sourced and excluded from the evidential base in one move — the form
  every later mention must repeat.
- **The relevance argument does not rest on automation being undemonstrated.** It rests on the *conditions*
  of the demonstrations: reported performance is unevenly robust (recurrent overfitting, class imbalance,
  weak early-grade detection; a reproduction that did not recover the original operating characteristics),
  and acquisition variability is measured and extensive. The pivot sentence: *"the settings with the greatest
  need are the settings whose images least resemble those on which the demonstrations were performed."*
- **P1/P2 pre-introduced in exactly one paragraph**, with the consequence made explicit rather than left
  implicit: if P2 is correct, *"a model reported without its preprocessing specified is a model reported
  incompletely, and comparisons between such models are comparisons between partly unknown systems."*
  Grounds delegated to §1.4.
- **Answerability argued separately from importance** — corpora with device provenance, independent grading
  and pixel-level annotation make a *controlled* contrast possible; OD-6 makes the deployment envelope
  specifiable rather than assumed.
- **The standing boundary placed at the outset:** decision support in a physician-in-the-loop paradigm; no
  clinical validity, regulatory compliance or deployment readiness anywhere in the work.

**Terms introduced:** paradigm **P1** (end-to-end CNN paradigm); paradigm **P2** (integrated
preprocessing-CNN paradigm); *resource-limited environment* (OD-6, in its four-condition operational form).

**Argument thread:** A necessary screening task, a capacity constraint that forces automation, a technical
capability whose demonstrations were obtained under conditions unlike the deployment context, and a
methodological question — how the model is specified — that the field has left open. The argument now needs
a goal that answers precisely that question.

**Final topic:** The standing boundary — decision support, not a diagnostic device.
