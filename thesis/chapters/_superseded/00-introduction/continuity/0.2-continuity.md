# CONTINUITY NOTE — §0.2 Scientific Novelty

**Version:** 7.1.0 · Follows §0.6. Hands off to §0.8 (Provisions Submitted for Defence).

---

**Key concepts established:**
- **Novelty separated from strength in the opening sentence** — kind, not magnitude; strength is §0.8's.
  This separation is what allows the two sections to sit beside each other without collision, and it must be
  preserved if either is revised.
- **The principal contribution is conceptual and is stated before the list** — the P1 → P2 reframing, with
  the reason that grounds it (the transform before the first convolution determines the feature space) and
  the **dual character** it confers on every item: an engineering result *and* evidence about a
  methodological stance.
- **Ten items**, of which three cannot be derived from any pre-existing project document:
  - **item 5, direct measurement of domain-shift reduction** — the mechanism measured rather than inferred
    from external accuracy, with both design features that carry the novelty: source-domain normalisation
    statistics (so the measurement is a property of the preprocessing, not of a fitting procedure) and
    **prospectively recorded falsifiability** (a reversal was live by construction and would have been a
    finding);
  - **item 7, in-domain initialisation admitted by an acceptance gate, including the negative result** —
    from-scratch label-free self-supervision failed the gate, reported as evidence with the reason it is
    retained: *"the gate exists precisely so that an initialisation may fail it"*;
  - **item 9, the defect shared by three transfer measures** — analytic, and **strictly descriptive**:
    *"it rehabilitates no result of this work, and no claim in this dissertation is rescued by it."*
- **The ablation item carries its fence in the same breath** (item 6): decomposition under a single
  initialisation does not retroactively convert the factorial into a single-factor manipulation.
- **SIR-4/SIR-5 handled at item 3**: the CLAHE formulation *extends* previously published own work rather
  than originating here, and the overlapping self-records are referred to in the singular so they cannot
  read as two independent precedents.
- **SB-1.5 and SB-4.1 attached at their only mentions** (item 10), so the two non-empirical contributions
  are bounded where they are introduced.
- **The closing denial** — no superiority over a named system, no clinical validity or deployment readiness,
  and no attribution of an improvement to preprocessing in isolation.

**Terms introduced:** none new; the pipeline elements and ALO appear at the level of nomenclature fixed in
§0.3 and §0.6.

**Argument thread:** The propositions are on the table with their criteria (§0.6) and the inventory of what
is new is complete (§0.2). The Introduction must now state what is actually **defended**, and at what
strength — which is the one place in Chapter 0 where outcomes are permitted, and where every fence carried
from Chapter 5 has to survive compression.

**Final topic:** What the novelty inventory does not assert.

**Setup for §0.8 (Provisions Submitted for Defence) — the chapter's single highest-risk section:**
- **Sources:** `results/tables/TAB-5.2_claim_strength.md` and §5.2.2 for the levels and the qualification
  each carries; `ARGUMENT_MAP.md` for the PC register; `continuity/5.C-continuity.md` for the position
  ceiling. Nothing may be promoted above the level §5.2.2 assigned.
- **A provision is not a result.** Each item is phrased as a proposition submitted for defence, in a form
  the candidate can be questioned on — not as a reported measurement.
- **A provision that omits its fence is a defect.** This is the rule stated in the §5.C hand-off and it is
  the acceptance criterion for the section. Specifically:
  - the dominance provision is about the **integrated configuration**, differing from baseline in
    initialisation as well as preprocessing (CFC-2.8), *and* the ablation decomposition is stated without
    dissolving that fence;
  - the stage-hierarchy provision holds at **grouping resolution only**, never as a strict ordering;
  - the transfer and device provisions must state that **both arms cleared their thresholds**, so the
    evidence lies in the comparison and in the reduction of spread rather than in the criterion;
  - the attention provision is bounded by NC-14, rests on one annotated corpus and one fold, and its
    **qualitative half was not evaluated**;
  - the external-performance provision is about **absolute performance, not resistance to degradation**, and
    its second corpus passed by a thin margin whose lower bound lies below the minimal important difference;
  - the domain-distance provision is **direction only** — magnitude does not track transfer gain;
  - the small-data result is **comparable to, not larger than**, the abundant-data gain;
  - two of the five camera groupings **are** the external clinical corpora and are not independent replication.
- **Three provisions are non-empirical** (the paradigmatic framing, the thermal-optical model, the
  architecture) and must be submitted as such — a design or theoretical provision defended as an empirical
  one is a defect of a different kind.
- **The PC register skips PC-3**, which is not in use; do not renumber to close the gap, and do not resurrect
  the demoted two-stage fine-tuning claim as a provision.
- Target 700–900 words. Given the chapter's overrun pattern the fences must be paid for out of the
  connective prose, not out of the qualifications.
