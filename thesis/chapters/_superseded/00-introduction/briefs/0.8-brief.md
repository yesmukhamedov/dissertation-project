### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Section objective:** Put on the table exactly what may be contested at the defence — no more, and nothing
stripped of its condition.

**Argumentative spine:**
- **Opening (short).** What a provision is here: a proposition submitted for contestation, classified at a
  level assigned against a promotion condition fixed before its experiment was run, and carrying a
  qualification that is not detachable from it. **A provision stated without its qualification is a
  different and weaker proposition, and would be defended under false pretences.**
- **Eleven provisions**, grouped by kind and numbered, each with its register identifier in parentheses:
  - **Conceptual (1).** *(PC-0)* Preprocessing is a formalisable and testable component of the diagnostic
    model rather than ancillary data preparation — the P1 → P2 reframing. Non-empirical: no experiment
    promotes or refutes it; the empirical results are *consistent with* it under the tested conditions and
    do not universally establish it (CFC-2.1).
  - **Empirical (2–9), each STRONG and each fenced.**
    2. *(PC-1)* Dominance of the integrated configuration over the baseline, on both architectures, under
       the conjunctive criterion, surviving correction for multiplicity and showing no interaction with
       architecture. **Fence:** the arms differ in initialisation as well as in preprocessing, so the
       proposition is about the *configuration*; the ablation decomposes that composite but does not
       dissolve it.
    3. *(PC-2)* Both photometric parameters exhibit interior optima within the tested ranges, confirmed on
       held-out data. **Fence:** bounded to the ranges tested; the values are not portable across corpora.
    4. *(PC-8)* The stage contributions are separable and orderable, with the photometric pair leading.
       **Fence: grouping resolution only** — adjacent ranks lie within noise, and no strict ordering of the
       stages is defended; the mask channel was not isolated.
    5. *(PC-11)* The integrated configuration reduces source-to-target distance in feature space on every
       external corpus examined. **Fence: direction only** — magnitude does not track performance gain;
       mechanistic, and measured in each arm's own representation space.
    6. *(PC-6)* Zero-shot transfer clears the pre-registered generalisation threshold and exceeds the
       baseline on every grade. **Fence:** the baseline clears the threshold too, so the criterion does not
       discriminate between the arms — the evidence is in the comparison.
    7. *(PC-7)* Model attention aligns better with expert lesion annotation under the integrated
       configuration, on all annotated lesion types and on both measures. **Fence:** alignment, not
       localisation (NC-14); one annotated corpus and one fold; **the qualitative half was not evaluated.**
    8. *(PC-9)* Performance is maintained across all camera groupings and, substantively, the dispersion
       across them is reduced. **Fence:** both arms clear the floor; two of the five groupings *are* the
       external clinical corpora and are not independent replication; not device certification (NC-16).
    9. *(PC-10)* Higher absolute weighted F1 on both external clinical corpora, each meeting the minimal
       clinically important difference with its interval excluding zero. **Fence: performance, not
       resistance to degradation**; the criterion does not aggregate; and the second corpus passed by
       **0.0041**, with its interval's lower bound below the minimal important difference. The thinness is
       stated here rather than left for a reader to compute.
  - **Methodological (10).** The defect shared by the degradation quantity, the generalisation ratio and the
    retention ratio: each normalises external performance against an arm's own in-domain performance and so
    penalises in-domain strength. **Fence: strictly descriptive — it rehabilitates no result of this work.**
  - **Design and theoretical (11).** *(PC-4)* the coupled thermal-optical model and *(PC-5)* the modular
    screening architecture, both submitted as what they are — theoretical and design contributions, not
    empirical ones (SB-1.5, SB-4.1).
- **One short paragraph on the small-data result**, which is submitted as an observation rather than as a
  provision: the gain is **comparable to, not larger than**, the abundant-data gain, and the corpus cannot
  be redistributed (SB-2.2).
- **Close.** What is *not* submitted: clinical validity, deployment readiness, device certification, lesion
  localisation, superiority over any published system, and extension beyond the corpora and hardware used.
  Plus the honest note on the uniformity of the levels — it follows from how narrowly each proposition is
  stated and from conditions fixed in advance, and is not an assessment of the work's importance.
