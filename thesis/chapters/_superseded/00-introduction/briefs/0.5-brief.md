### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Section objective:** State the object and the subject so precisely that the scope of every later claim can
be tested against them.

**Argumentative spine:**
- **Object (one short paragraph).** The *process* of automated multi-stage diabetic retinopathy diagnosis
  from colour fundus photographs by convolutional neural networks. Name the material of that process — the
  tiered corpus of eight datasets, public and clinical, across four camera manufacturers — but keep material
  and object distinct: a list of datasets is not a definition.
- **Subject (one short paragraph).** Methods and models for integrating fundus image preprocessing with CNN
  classification, and the properties of the resulting integrated configuration: diagnostic performance under
  five-class grading, transferability to corpora not seen in training, the alignment of model attention with
  annotated pathology, and behaviour across camera domains. State the relation plainly — the object is what
  the work studies, the subject is what it manipulates within the object.
- **One closing sentence** fixing the boundary: fundus photography only (SB-1.4), five-class grading, and the
  named corpora — so that "outside the object" and "outside the subject" are both testable, not rhetorical.
