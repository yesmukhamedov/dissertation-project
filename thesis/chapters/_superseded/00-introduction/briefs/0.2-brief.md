### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Section objective:** Enumerate what did not exist before this work, at the level of *kind* rather than
*strength*, so that §0.8 can then say how far each is defended.

**Argumentative spine:**
- **Opening (short).** Distinguish novelty from result: this section states what is new, §0.8 states what is
  defended and at what strength. Then the conceptual claim that organises the rest — the principal
  contribution is the reframing of preprocessing from ancillary data preparation (P1) to an integral model
  component (P2), and every item below has a dual character: an engineering result on the pipeline, and
  evidence for the productivity of P2 as a methodological stance.
- **The novelty items**, each one short paragraph or numbered clause:
  1. **Preprocessing formalised as part of the model specification** and placed under *controlled
     experimental contrast* rather than argued discursively — the operationalisation of P2. This is the
     item the others serve.
  2. **The engineering realisation of the pipeline**: isotropic resize with centred zero-padding preserving
     fundus circle geometry; the binary field-of-view mask as an explicit stage and fourth input channel;
     adaptive flat-field correction whose σ scales with per-image field-of-view diameter rather than a
     fixed global value, applied inside the mask only; dataset-specific normalisation computed from valid
     fundus pixels rather than natural-image defaults; canonical orientation with per-image adaptive
     augmentation σ derived from landmark-localisation uncertainty.
  3. **The dual-constraint stochastic CLAHE** in the LAB colour space, with the clip limit as the minimum of
     two constraints and stochastic train-time application serving additionally as regularisation —
     **adapted and extended from the candidate's own prior publications (SIR-4), not presented as new
     ex nihilo**, and validated here in the five-class context.
  4. **Attention–Lesion Overlap as a primary, asymmetric explainability metric**, measuring the fraction of
     an annotated lesion covered by model attention, with Intersection-over-Union secondary — compared per
     lesion type against pixel-level annotation. NC-14 travels with it.
  5. **Direct measurement of domain-shift reduction (SC-I)** — the item the outline cannot supply. Most work
     asserting that preprocessing improves cross-domain robustness demonstrates it through its
     *consequence*, external accuracy, leaving the postulated mechanism unmeasured; here the mechanism is
     measured, at the penultimate layer, across six corpora, at the cost of forward passes only. Two design
     features carry the novelty: source-domain normalisation statistics, which make the measurement a
     property of the preprocessing rather than of a fitting procedure; and its **prospectively recorded
     falsifiability** — a reversal was a live possibility by construction, and would have been a finding.
  6. **The cumulative stage ablation under a single initialisation**, which decomposes an effect that the
     factorial design necessarily confounds — **with its fence in the same clause**: it does not retroactively
     convert the factorial into a single-factor manipulation.
  7. **In-domain initialisation selected by a linear-probe acceptance gate** — including the **negative
     result**, that from-scratch label-free CNN self-supervision failed the gate on this corpus, which is
     recorded as evidence rather than omitted. A novelty section that reports a failed branch is more
     credible, not less.
  8. **The tiered multi-corpus, multi-device validation architecture** as a design object in its own right:
     eight corpora organised by function across four camera manufacturers and several grading protocols.
  9. **The secondary methodological contribution**: identification of a defect shared by three
     transfer measures in common use — the degradation quantity, the generalisation ratio and the retention
     ratio all normalise external performance against an arm's own in-domain performance, and therefore
     penalise in-domain strength. **Strictly descriptive; it rehabilitates no result of this work.**
  10. **Two contributions that are non-empirical by design**: the coupled thermal-optical model of fundus
      tissue response (theoretical and computational only, SB-1.5) and the modular screening-system
      architecture (design specification only, SB-4.1). Both bounded at their only mention.
- **Close (short).** What the list does *not* assert — no novelty item is a claim of superiority over any
  published system, of clinical validity, or of an isolated preprocessing effect.
