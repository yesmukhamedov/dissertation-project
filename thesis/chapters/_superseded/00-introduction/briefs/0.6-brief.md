### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Section objective:** Present the hypothesis register as an argument, not a list — the central hypothesis
first, the seven as its decompositions, each with its acceptance criterion and its bound.

**Argumentative spine:**
- **Opening: the central unifying hypothesis**, in the register's own terms — the pipeline reduces domain
  variability across devices and acquisition conditions while preserving diagnostically relevant features,
  leading to improved CNN-based detection. Then the structural point that organises everything after it:
  the seven are decompositions of that claim, and **H-3 tests its first clause directly** while the others
  approach it through its consequence. Stating this makes the register read as a causal chain with a
  measurable middle term rather than as seven unrelated bets.
- **H-1 through H-7, in register order**, each in one compact paragraph: the conditional, the acceptance
  criterion, and the bound that travels with it.
  - **H-1** — conjunctive EH-3 criterion (weighted F1 ≥ 5 pp **and** ROC-AUC ≥ 0.02 **and** no κ
    degradation, all three simultaneously), replicated across both architectures per EH-4. **The composite
    independent variable belongs inside the statement**: the arms differ jointly in preprocessing and in
    initialisation, so CFC-2.8 is a property of the hypothesis as posed, not a caveat added afterwards.
  - **H-2** — at least one identifiable optimum within the tested parameter range; bounded to that range,
    no extrapolation (CFC-1.2, DGL-5).
  - **H-3** — the full acceptance form: Δd(X) = d(baseline, X) − d(integrated, X), PASS ⟺ Δd ≥ MCID_d = 0.0
    ∧ CI⁻(Δd) > 0, satisfied on at least K = 5 of n = 6 corpora; MMD over penultimate-layer features
    primary, KL over channel histograms secondary and informational only. **Three things must appear
    here and nowhere else:** the *label-reuse notice* (the H-3 dropped in V3 was a training-method
    hypothesis; the label is reused, the retirement stands); the *mandatory protocol condition* (Stage 7
    uses source-domain statistics, or the measurement is target adaptation and incomparable with
    H-4/H-6/H-7); and the *threshold provenance* — MCID_d and K were assigned at formalisation, not
    pre-registered, with the two facts that bound the significance of that.
  - **H-4** — G = F1_APTOS / F1_EyePACS ≥ 0.85, zero-shot, no retraining; the threshold is a
    pre-registered success criterion, not a guaranteed outcome.
  - **H-5** — ALO primary, IoU secondary, against pixel-level lesion annotation; the qualitative overlays
    on the clinical corpus are part of what the hypothesis contemplates and must be named as such;
    bounded by NC-14 in the statement.
  - **H-6** — performance maintained across camera domains, DR labels only; NC-16 in the statement.
  - **H-7** — per-corpus form: PASS ⟺ Δ wF1(X) ≥ MCID 0.050 ∧ CI⁻ > 0, on **each** of two corpora,
    **not aggregated**, a reversal on either yielding REVERSED. Two precision points that are routinely
    lost: the form requires CI⁻ > 0 and **not** CI⁻ ≥ MCID; and the hypothesis claims **higher absolute
    external performance, not reduced degradation** — the retired Δ_drop form is named as retired and as
    descriptive only.
- **Close:** every criterion was fixed before its experiment; the register is what makes the work falsifiable
  rather than merely reportable; outcomes are given in §0.8 and in Chapters 4–5, not here.

**Required tables/figures:** none. Criteria are given inline in prose and light notation.

### BOUNDARY WARNINGS

1. **Criteria, never outcomes.** Not one verdict, not one measured value, not one strength level. §5.2.2's
   whole argument is that promotion conditions were written before the results existed; an Introduction that
   leaks outcomes into the hypothesis register destroys that argument retroactively.
2. **CFC-2.8 inside the H-1 statement**, phrased as a property of the design. "The effect is attributable to
   the integrated configuration" is right; "…but note that pretraining also differs" as an afterthought is wrong.
3. **H-3's label reuse must be stated**, because §2.3.2 and §3.3.3 say "H-3 dropped" and a reader meeting the
   label here first would otherwise be misled by the later chapters.
4. **H-3's threshold provenance must be stated.** Concealing that MCID_d and K were assigned at formalisation
   would misrepresent the register and would be the one place in this section where the work could be accused
   of fitting a criterion to a result.
5. **H-7 in the External Clinical Performance form only.** The Δ_drop form appears once, named as retired.
6. **No hedging into vagueness.** A criterion that cannot be checked is not a criterion.
7. **Rule 16** — no process history.
