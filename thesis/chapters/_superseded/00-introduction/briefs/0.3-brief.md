### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Section objective:** Convert the methodological gap of §0.1 into a single, checkable goal.

**Argumentative spine:**
- **Thesis (one sentence, the goal proper):** to develop and experimentally validate an integrated fundus
  image enhancement and CNN classification framework for automated five-class DR diagnosis, in which an
  8-stage preprocessing pipeline is specified as an integral component of the model, and to establish under
  controlled contrast what difference that specification makes.
- **Then three clarifying clauses, each doing work the headline sentence cannot:**
  1. **What "integrated" commits to** — the pipeline is part of the model specification, so the unit of
     evaluation is the configuration, not the network; and the contrast is run under matched conditions
     (same corpus, same partitions, same budget, same hardware).
  2. **What validation covers** — five directions, named as a programme and not as findings: in-domain
     contrast and stage decomposition; direct measurement of source-to-target distance in feature space;
     transfer without retraining; alignment of model attention with expert lesion annotation; behaviour
     across external clinical corpora and across camera groupings, including in a data-scarce regime.
  3. **Under what constraints** — the resource-limited computational envelope (OD-6), which is a condition
     of the goal rather than an afterthought to it.
- **Close:** the boundary carried from §0.1 — what achieving the goal would and would not establish.

**Required tables/figures:** none.
