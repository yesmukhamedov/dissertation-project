### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Section objective:** Report the measured distance reductions, state the H-3 verdict, extract the three structural observations that make the result informative beyond its direction, and — with equal weight — establish precisely what the result does **not** support.

**Argumentative spine (required):**
- **Thesis:** The pipeline moves every target domain closer to the source, on both measures, without exception and with statistical separation — which establishes the mechanism the earlier sections could only infer. But the *structure* of that reduction shows it to be a proportional compression of photometric spread rather than a removal of domain difference, and its magnitude does not track the magnitude of the transfer gain; both facts constrain how the mechanism may be invoked in later chapters.
- **Reasoning chain:**
  1. Open from §4.4.1: protocol fixed, caveats stated; the measurements follow.
  2. Render **TAB-4.11** inline: per domain, MMD baseline → integrated with Δd and its 95 % CI, and KL baseline → integrated with the percentage reduction.
  3. **Verdict:** the distance falls for all six domains on both measures; Δd spans +0.0700 to +0.0931 and **all six confidence intervals exclude zero**; KL falls by 34–38 %. H-3 is supported. Note that direction consistency across six independent target corpora is itself the strongest feature of the result — a six-for-six outcome is not readily produced by chance.
  4. **Structural observation 1 — proportional compression.** The KL reduction sits in a narrow band (34–38 %) *regardless of how remote the domain was to begin with*. Draw the inference: the pipeline compresses the photometric spread by a roughly fixed proportion rather than pulling distant domains up to the level of near ones. This is what one would expect of a fixed transform applied identically to every domain, and it is inconsistent with any reading of the pipeline as adaptive.
  5. **Structural observation 2 — the ranking is preserved.** RFMiD remains the most distant and Messidor-2 the closest, before and after. Inference: the residual difference between corpora is *substantive* — population composition, imaging protocol, disease prevalence — and does not reduce to illumination and contrast. This is an important honesty point: the pipeline does not make domains equivalent.
  6. **Structural observation 3 — no target adaptation.** Restate briefly from §4.4.1: source-domain normalization statistics, so Stages 0–6 carry the convergence.
  7. **The negative result, given its own paragraph and full weight.** Across the six domains, the size of the MMD reduction does **not** track the size of the transfer gain: RFMiD matches at the top (largest Δd +0.0931, largest gain +0.0987), but IDRiD is 2nd on Δd and 4th on gain, DDR is 4th on Δd yet has the *smallest* gain of all six, and APTOS is 5th on Δd with the 2nd-largest gain. Rank correlation ρ ≈ 0.49. State explicitly: **direction only may be reported; the magnitude correspondence may not.** The association is over six points and is not causation.
  8. Bound it: DGL-1; the MMD model-dependence limit; NC-16; no clinical inference; the `[VERIFY]` protocol gaps from §4.4.1 affect interval widths, not direction.
  9. Cite **FIG-4.17** as `ASSET TO BE CREATED`.
- **Conclusion / hand-off:** The mechanism postulated by the central thesis is measured rather than assumed — the pipeline demonstrably narrows the gap between corpora, in the representation the classifier uses, without seeing the target. §4.5 through §4.9 test the consequences that ought to follow.

### BOUNDARY WARNINGS

1. **The retired formulation "the smallest distance reduction corresponds to the smallest gain" is FALSE on this evidence and must not be written.** The smallest gain is DDR's, at a middling Δd.
2. **Report direction, not magnitude correspondence.** ρ ≈ 0.49 over six points.
3. **No causal claim.** Distance reduction is consistent with the transfer results; it is not shown to produce them.
4. **The pipeline does not make domains equivalent** — say so via the preserved ranking.
5. **NC-16 / CFC-2.4:** no certification, no clinical inference.

### COUNTER-ARGUMENT & INHERITED LIMITATIONS

- **Counter-position 1:** "convergence was manufactured by fitting normalization to the target." Refuted by the source-statistics design (§4.4.1), restated here.
