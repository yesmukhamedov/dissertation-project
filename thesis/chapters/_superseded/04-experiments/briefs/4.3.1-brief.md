### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Section objective:** Specify the eight-level cumulative ablation, report the per-level weighted-F1 contributions, establish that the endpoints reproduce Experiment 1 under a single initialization — thereby decomposing the composite factor — and state the stage hierarchy at exactly the resolution the evidence supports.

**Argumentative spine (required):**
- **Thesis:** Holding the initialization constant and adding the stages one at a time in pipeline order reproduces the entire Experiment-1 gain, which shows the preprocessing contribution to be separable from the initialization contribution; and the resulting per-level increments are all individually significant, with the two photometric stages carrying the largest share — a grouping the data resolve, though not a strict rank order.
- **Reasoning chain:**
  1. Open from the §4.2.3 verdict and the debt it left: Experiment 1 licensed a claim about the integrated configuration as a unitary system. This section performs the separation.
  2. State the design precisely — and state *why* each design choice is what makes the decomposition valid: same corpus (EyePACS 100 %, n = 35 126), same patient-level fold assignment, one backbone (EfficientNet-B3), and **a single initialization at every level**. The last is the crux: with initialization held fixed, the only quantity varying across levels is the preprocessing content.
  3. Define the levels: L0 = baseline; L1 + Stage 0; L2 + Stage 1; L3 + Stages 2–3; L4 + Stage 4; L5 + Stage 5; L6 + Stage 6; L7 + Stage 7. Note that levels are *cumulative*, so Δⱼ is the contribution of stage j **given** stages 0…j−1.
  4. Render **TAB-4.4** inline (level, stages, wF1, ROC-AUC, κ, accuracy, σ_fold, |Δⱼ|, 2·σ_fold, significance).
  5. **The decomposition, stated as the section's headline:** L0 = 0.7538 and L7 = 0.8193 coincide numerically with configurations C and D; cumulative ΔwF1 = +0.0655 matches the +6.55 pp of Experiment 1. Draw the inference carefully and bound it (see BOUNDARY WARNINGS 1).
  6. Render the per-fold table (or state its content): monotonic L0 < L1 < … < L7 **in every one of the five folds, with no inversion** — a much stronger statement than monotonicity on the mean.
  7. Significance: all seven |Δⱼ| exceed 2·σ_fold (0.0065–0.0143 against 0.0042–0.0060) → 7 of 7, no stage redundant. **Name the heuristic as a heuristic.**
  8. The hierarchy, at the right resolution: rank table with shares; flat-field 0.0143 (22 %) and CLAHE 0.0125 (19 %) lead, together **41 %** — approximately as much as the four geometric and normalization stages combined. Then the honest qualifier: the spread of Δⱼ (0.0078) is ≈3·σ_fold, so what the data resolve is the **grouping** (photometric above the rest), while adjacent ranks sit within noise. Do not present a strict 1-to-7 ordering as established.
  9. Metric consistency: κ rises most (+0.1103), AUC least (+0.0360) — the same ratio as Experiment 1, consistent with distant-error removal (§4.2.3).
  10. Limitations, stated in the body: (i) fixed stage order → interactions unmeasured, Δⱼ is order-dependent; (ii) **Stage 3 (FOV mask) is not isolated** — L3 adds Stages 2 and 3 jointly, so rank 4 belongs to the pair; (iii) NC-17 — no universal optimality of the stage set.
- **Conclusion / hand-off:** The component half of H-2 is established; §4.3.2 and §4.3.3 test the parametric half — whether the two leading photometric stages exhibit interior optima in their controlling parameters.

### BOUNDARY WARNINGS

1. **The decomposition claim must be phrased exactly.** Admissible: *"under a single initialization held constant at every level, the cumulative addition of the eight stages reproduces the full Experiment-1 gain, so the preprocessing contribution is separable from the initialization contribution."* **Not** admissible: *"preprocessing alone explains the Experiment-1 result"* — Experiment 1 remains a composite manipulation and this experiment does not retroactively change it.
2. **The 2·σ_fold rule is a heuristic.** Say so. It is not a paired significance test.
3. **Do not assert a strict 1-to-7 stage ranking.** The grouping is what the data resolve.
4. **Stage 3 is not isolated** — state it as a limitation, not a footnote.
5. **NC-17 / CFC-2.1:** neither the stage set nor its order is claimed optimal or universal.

### COUNTER-ARGUMENT & INHERITED LIMITATIONS
