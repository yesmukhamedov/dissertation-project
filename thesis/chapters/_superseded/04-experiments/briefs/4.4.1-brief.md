### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Section objective:** State what is measured, on what objects, between which domains, and — critically — why the design does not permit the reduction to be an artifact of fitting the normalization to the target data.

**Argumentative spine (required):**
- **Thesis:** The claim that preprocessing reduces domain variability is testable independently of any classification outcome, and the protocol tests it at two levels that can fail independently — the pixel level, where the transform acts, and the representational level, where the network sees.
- **Reasoning chain:**
  1. Open from §4.3.3's closing question: the gain has been localized in the photometric stages but not explained; classification and image-quality metrics both leave the mechanism unmeasured. H-3 states the mechanism and this section states how it is measured.
  2. State H-3 plainly: the pipeline reduces the distance between the source domain (EyePACS) and target domains, at both the pixel and the representational level.
  3. **Two measures, two levels, and why both are needed.** KL divergence over per-channel intensity histograms measures convergence at the level the transform directly manipulates — a near-tautology risk that must be acknowledged. MMD over penultimate-layer features measures convergence in the space the classifier actually uses, which is not entailed by the pixel-level result: a transform can homogenize intensities while leaving representations as far apart as before. The two can disagree, and that is the point of measuring both.
  4. **The mandatory caveat on MMD.** It is computed over penultimate-layer features, which are a property of the model. The baseline and integrated distances are therefore measured in *different* feature spaces, and what is compared is the relative remoteness of a target domain within each arm's own representation, not two distances in one shared space. This is standard practice in the domain-shift literature but it is a real interpretive limit and must be stated here, at the protocol, not deferred.
  5. **The design property that forecloses the obvious objection.** Stage 7 normalization uses **source-domain statistics**; target domains do **not** recompute their own. Any convergence measured is therefore achieved by Stages 0–6 and is not a covert form of target-domain adaptation. This is the single most important sentence in the section.
  6. Name the six target domains (APTOS, IDRiD, Messidor-2, DDR, ODIR-5K, RFMiD) and note that they span the external, clinical and device tiers of §4.1.1, so the measurement is not confined to one kind of shift.
  7. **State the pre-specified reversal case.** Stages 5 and 7 are source-bound by construction, so a reversal — variability reduced *within* the source domain, increased *across* domains — was a live possibility and would have been an established finding, not a failed run. This is what makes the hypothesis falsifiable rather than a formality.
  8. State the flagged gap honestly: the kernel choice and per-domain sample size are flagged for verification; the direction and significance of the reported differences do not depend on them, but full reproducibility of the interval widths does.
- **Conclusion / hand-off:** §4.4.2 reports the measurements and the limits of what they support.
