### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Objective:** Report camera-wise performance, adjudicate H-6, establish the spread reduction as the substantive result, and explain the retention-ratio inversions as a denominator artifact — the third instance of one structural defect.

**Argumentative spine:**
- **Thesis:** Both arms clear the floor everywhere, so the threshold is not the finding; the finding is that between-group variability falls by roughly a factor of 2.4–3.1, because the integrated configuration lifts the *worst* hardware groups disproportionately — which is what a fixed-proportion photometric transform would predict and what matters most for deployment across heterogeneous hardware.
- **Chain:**
  1. Motivate: earlier corpora confound population, protocol and hardware; this experiment partitions by imaging hardware.
  2. **Disclose the §4.7 overlap BEFORE the table** — Kowa/Topcon groups *are* IDRiD/Messidor-2; values coincide by construction; not independent replication. The informative content is the comparison *across* groups.
  3. State the floor criterion.
  4. Render TAB-4.9 with per-group Δ, CIs and retention ratios.
  5. Verdict: 5/5 both arms → H-6 supported; **immediately qualify** — baseline clears everywhere too, so the threshold does not discriminate; the discriminating result is that absolute wF1 is higher on every group (+0.0517…+0.0987), all CIs excluding zero. (Same handling as §4.5.1.)
  6. **The spread result, with its clinical rationale argued:** std(wF1) 0.0306 → 0.0130 (−2.4×), std(AUC) 0.0214 → 0.0070 (−3.1×), both CIs excluding zero. Mechanism: largest gain where baseline weakest (RFMiD +0.0987 from 0.5434), smallest where baseline strongest (DDR). The range is **compressed, not shifted**. Argue why variability matters clinically: uniform performance and mean-equal-but-uneven performance carry different risk.
  7. **The retention-ratio inversions, explained not omitted.** g falls on DDR (0.816 → 0.814) and Messidor-2 (0.833 → 0.833) while absolute wF1 *rises* on both with CIs excluding zero. Cause: the integrated arm's larger own-in-domain denominator (0.8193 vs 0.7538); an arm improving in-domain must improve proportionally more externally merely to hold its ratio. **Same defect as §4.5.1's G and §4.7's Δ_drop — third guise.** Denominator artifact, not a performance finding; absolute figures are what is reported.
  8. Limitations: §4.7 overlap → fewer than five distinct settings; three groups are "mixed" aggregates → proxy for hardware, not per-device stratification; fold-0; **per-group confusion matrices not recorded** → error structure within groups unexaminable, routed to §5.4.
  9. **NC-16 in its own closing paragraph**, affirmatively.
- **Hand-off:** §4.9 tests the data-scarce regime.

**Required elements:** overlap disclosure before the table; floor criterion; TAB-4.9; verdict + both-arms-clear qualification; spread table with clinical rationale and mechanism; retention-ratio explanation linked to §4.5.1/§4.7; four limitations; NC-16 paragraph.
