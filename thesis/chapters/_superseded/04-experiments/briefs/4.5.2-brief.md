### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Objective:** Show *where* the transfer advantage arises — per grade, in the error structure, and at the referral threshold — and connect the result to §4.4 in both its supporting and its cautionary aspect.

**Argumentative spine:**
- **Thesis:** What survives the domain change is not uniform accuracy but the **ordinal coherence** of the model's output; that property is what produces the large κ gain and what carries the improvement at the referral threshold, which is where a screening system's value actually lies.
- **Chain:**
  1. Open by naming the distinction that matters: an advantage in the majority grade would be of limited clinical interest; one in the grades governing referral would not.
  2. Render per-class F1 inline. All five grades higher; largest relative gains DR1 (×1.95) and DR3 (×1.33) — the two weakest baseline grades. macro-F1 rises more than weighted, as in-domain. **State the residual absolute weakness** (DR1 0.2720, DR3 0.3252 — improvements on a low base, not solutions).
  3. Read the confusion structure with specific cells: DR2→DR1 245 → 192, DR2→DR0 96 → 33 (roughly a third of moderate disease under-graded in baseline, much of it below the referral threshold); severe under-grading near-eliminated (DR3→DR0 10 → 1, DR4→DR0 6 → 0); over-calling reduced (DR0→≥DR2 49 → 16). Derive **ordinal coherence** as the operative property, and tie it to κ 0.7887 → 0.8874.
  4. Render referable-threshold metrics inline. Sens +10.6 pp **with** Spec +2.0 pp → curve shift, not threshold shift (same argument as §4.2.3, now under transfer). Referable AUC +0.040. PPV +4.3 pp, NPV +6.1 pp → the gain is not bought by referring indiscriminately. Note the baseline's 0.894 is not a failure — this keeps the comparison honest.
  5. **Two connections to §4.4, one supporting and one cautionary.** Supporting: APTOS's distance fell and its performance rose — the qualitative prediction holds. Cautionary: APTOS ranked **5th of six on distance reduction but 2nd on gain**, which is exactly why the magnitude caveat was stated firmly. Give the reason (class balance, grading conventions, prevalence of the model's weak grades) and restate that the mechanism is one operative factor, not the sole or proportional driver.
  6. Carry the §4.5.1 limitations unchanged: fold-0, DGL-1, CFC-2.8.
- **Hand-off:** the first of five consequence tests is met; §4.6–§4.9 test the remaining four.

**Required elements:** per-class table; residual-weakness statement; confusion structure with specific cells; ordinal-coherence derivation tied to κ; referable table with the curve-shift argument; the supporting/cautionary §4.4 connections; limitations carried.
