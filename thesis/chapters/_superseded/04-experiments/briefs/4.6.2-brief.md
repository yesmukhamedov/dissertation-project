### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Objective:** Report the overlap comparison on both measures, establish robustness and informativeness, and close by stating precisely what the result supports and what it does not.

**Argumentative spine:**
- **Thesis:** The integrated configuration's attention is better aligned with expert annotation on every lesion type, robustly to the threshold and on a substantially informative sample — a bounded claim about alignment that the design supports and that stops well short of localization.
- **Chain:**
  1. Render TAB-4.7 (ALO, primary) and the IoU table (secondary) inline with Δ, CI and p per type.
  2. State the verdict: 4/4 types, both measures, all CIs exclude zero, all p < 0.05 at τ = 0.5; effects +0.0992…+0.1288, roughly 40–50 % over baseline levels.
  3. **Do not double-count IoU.** Its agreement with ALO is *corroboration, not a second finding* — the measures are not independent constructions.
  4. **Two informative patterns:** (i) baseline overlap ordering tracks lesion conspicuity (hard exudates highest 0.3502, microaneurysms lowest 0.2126) — offer this as a **weak validity check**, at that strength only; (ii) the largest *relative* improvement falls where baseline is weakest (microaneurysms ≈49 % vs hard exudates ≈37 %), consistent with the DR1 pattern of §4.2.3/§4.5.2 — note the two are arguably measurements of the same phenomenon.
  5. Render the τ sweep. Direction 4/4 at every τ; significance 4/4 except at τ = 0.7 where it is 3/4. **Report the exception**, attribute it to reduced power on small samples (direction stays positive on all four), and give the accurate summary: not threshold-dependent within the range, but not uniformly significant across it either.
  6. Floor effect: zero overlap in both arms for 6/54 (f₀ = 0.111) → 48 informative pairs. The effect is not the product of a small informative subset.
  7. Arm classification under this backbone (wF1 0.7545 → 0.7766, AUC 0.8307 → 0.8542, κ 0.6602 → 0.7075) — included only to establish that the attention comparison is between models of comparable competence.
  8. **Closing paragraph: what is and is not supported.** Supported: better alignment, 4/4, threshold-robust, most pairs informative. Not supported: localization/detection/segmentation (NC-14); extrapolation beyond the corpus; precise magnitude per type (soft exudates n = 26).

**Required elements:** both tables; verdict; IoU-as-corroboration caveat; the two patterns; τ sweep with the τ = 0.7 exception; floor fraction; arm-comparability figures; the supported/not-supported closing.
