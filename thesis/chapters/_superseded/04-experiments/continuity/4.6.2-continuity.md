**Key concepts established:**
- **Table 4.21 (TAB-4.7, ALO — primary)** and **Table 4.22 (IoU — secondary)** inline, with Δ, 95 % CI and paired Wilcoxon p per lesion type. **4/4 types on both measures**, every CI excluding zero, every test significant at the 0.05 level at τ = 0.5. ALO effects **+0.0992 … +0.1288** (≈40–50 % over baseline levels).
- **IoU is NOT double-counted.** Explicit: the two measures are not independent constructions, so IoU's agreement *"is corroboration rather than a second finding."*
- **Pattern 1 — conspicuity ordering as a weak validity check.** Baseline overlap is highest for hard exudates (0.3502, bright and large) and lowest for microaneurysms (0.2126, small and low-contrast) — the ordering §1.1.1's morphology predicts. Offered *at that strength only*: an overlap statistic that did not respect conspicuity would be hard to interpret.
- **Pattern 2 — largest relative gain where baseline is weakest.** Microaneurysm overlap rises ≈49 % against ≈37 % for hard exudates, i.e. alignment improves most for the lesion class defining the earliest disease grade — **consistent with the DR1 classification pattern of §4.2.3 and §4.5.2**, and noted as arguably two measurements of the same phenomenon.
- **Table 4.23 (τ sweep).** Direction positive **4/4 at every τ**; significance 4/4 except at **τ = 0.7, where it is 3/4**. The exception is **reported, not omitted**, attributed to reduced power on small samples (direction stays positive on all four), with the accurate summary given: *"not threshold-dependent within the range examined, but not uniformly significant across it either, and the accurate statement is the second one."*
- **Floor effect: 6/54 = 0.111** zero-in-both → **48 informative pairs**. The effect is not the artifact of a small informative subset.
- **Arm comparability established, not claimed:** under this backbone wF1 0.7545 → 0.7766, AUC 0.8307 → 0.8542, κ 0.6602 → 0.7075 — included only so the attention comparison is not between a competent and an incompetent model.
- **Closing separates supported from unsupported.** Supported: better alignment, 4/4, threshold-robust, most pairs informative. **Not supported:** localization / detection / delineation (NC-14, restated at the conclusion); extrapolation beyond the corpus; precise per-type magnitude (soft exudates n = 26).
