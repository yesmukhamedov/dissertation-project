- **VERDICT — H-3 SUPPORTED against the formal criterion.** On the **primary** measure PASS_S holds for all six corpora (Δd ∈ [+0.0700, +0.0931], **all six CIs exclude zero**), so **Σ PASS_S = 6 ≥ K = 5**. On the **secondary, informational** measure KL falls 34–38 % on all six — reported for where the convergence originates, **forming no part of the verdict**.
- **THRESHOLD PROVENANCE ANSWERED IN THE BODY, not glossed.** The post-hoc assignment of MCID_d and K is raised together with the objection it invites and answered on two concrete grounds: (i) **MCID_d = 0.0 is not tuned** but the only interpretable choice for an unnormalized distance, leaving *"the weakest non-trivial condition available, not a favourable one"*; (ii) **the verdict is insensitive to K** — the outcome satisfies the rule for **every K from 1 to 6**, so no admissible choice would have changed it. The rule's one-corpus tolerance **was never exercised**.
- **The pre-specified reversal case is resolved in the negative, on the record:** the source-bound stages (5 and 7) did **not** push foreign corpora further away, so the reversal that would have explained a failure of the external evaluations did not arise.
- **Structural observation 1 — proportional, not levelling.** KL reduction sits in a narrow 34–38 % band irrespective of initial remoteness; RFMiD (most distant, 0.1370) and APTOS (among the nearest, 0.0894) **both fell 34 %**. Inference: the pipeline compresses photometric spread by a roughly fixed proportion — exactly what a fixed transform applied identically should do, and **inconsistent with any adaptive reading**.
- **Structural observation 2 — ranking preserved (a limitation, stated as one).** RFMiD stays most distant, Messidor-2 closest, intermediate order unchanged. *"The pipeline narrows the gap between corpora; it does not make them equivalent."* Residual difference is **substantive** — population composition, prevalence, acquisition protocol, field definition — and no amount of photometric normalization removes it. Explicit disclaimer: *"Any expectation that preprocessing alone renders a model corpus-agnostic is not supported by this measurement, and this dissertation makes no such claim."*
- **Structural observation 3 — no target adaptation**, restated from §4.4.1: source-domain statistics, Stages 0–6 carry the convergence.
- **THE NEGATIVE RESULT, given a full paragraph and not deflected.** Magnitude of distance reduction does **not** track magnitude of transfer gain. Extremes agree (RFMiD: largest Δd +0.0931, largest gain +0.0987) but below that the orderings diverge — **IDRiD 2nd on Δd / 4th on gain; DDR 4th on Δd yet the *smallest* gain of all six; APTOS 5th on Δd / 2nd on gain**. **ρ ≈ 0.49** over six points.
- **THE HONEST READING — fixed phrasing for reuse in §4.C, §5.4 and Chapter 7:** H-3 establishes **that the mechanism operates**, not that the size of the reduction determines or predicts the size of the gain. Cross-domain performance depends on more than distributional proximity (class balance, grading conventions, resolution, lesion prevalence), and six points cannot apportion those contributions. The result is reported as **directional evidence for the mechanism** and as **qualitative consistency** with the transfer results — *distance falls on every domain, performance rises on every domain* — **without** a causal or magnitude-predictive claim.
- **Cross-measure agreement used correctly:** because KL is model-independent and MMD is not, their agreement across all six domains is offered as *partial reassurance* that the representational result is not an artifact of differing feature spaces — **not** as a resolution of the model-dependence limit.
