**Key concepts established:**
- **Table 4.15 (FIG-4.10)** inline: σ ∈ [0.05, 0.10]·D_FOV, strictly unimodal, maximum at **σ = 0.07** with decline on both sides (0.7662 below, 0.7577 above). **R = 0.0512**, of the same order as the whole pipeline effect (+0.0655) — *"a poor choice of σ within an otherwise reasonable interval costs almost as much as omitting the pipeline altogether."*
- **Shape reading:** too small a σ makes the illumination estimate follow retinal structure and subtract signal; too large makes it too smooth to track vignetting. The optimum is where the estimate is *broad enough to be structure-blind and narrow enough to be illumination-sensitive*.
- **Validation, not tuning.** σ\* = 0.07·D_FOV is the value already fixed in §3.1.1 and used in every experiment in the chapter. The sweep confirms it on evidence; **no parameter changed, no other chapter result affected.** Held out: 0.7513 → **0.8087**, Δ **+0.0574**, CI **[+0.0428, +0.0806]**.
- **Within-stage CNR agreement:** CNR maximum (3.93) falls exactly at σ\*, and CNR ranking matches wF1 ranking at all six points — deliberately set up as the *contrast* for what follows.
- **Table 4.16 (TAB-4.5)** inline, read **level by level**:
  - L1–L3 (geometric): CNR 20.43 → 20.38, entropy 5.502 → 5.514 (within rounding); only SSIM falls (geometric transformation). Yet **+2.21 pp wF1** — a third of the eventual gain, invisible to the metrics.
  - L4 (flat-field): the only stage raising CNR substantially, 20.38 → **28.60** (+40 %); also the largest single contributor.
  - **L5 (CLAHE): *lowers* CNR (28.60 → 24.15) while producing the largest entropy jump (5.596 → 5.884)** — and is the 2nd-largest contributor at 19 %. **The sharpest counterexample to the proxy reading**, and it is used as such.
  - L6 (augmentation): metrics numerically unchanged — invisible **by construction** (train-time-only stage, quality measured on the validation configuration) — yet +0.0101 wF1.
  - SSIM falls monotonically 1.000 → 0.865 **while wF1 rises monotonically**: distance from the original frame is not degradation.
- **THE SYNTHESIS — 41 % / 49 %.** The only two levels that move the metrics (L4, L5) are exactly the two largest contributors, 41 % of the gain: the metrics are **not noise**, they flag the leading component, and the σ sweep shows they can track it finely when the variation is photometric. But **49 % of the gain arises where the metrics are flat or move the wrong way.** **Formulation fixed for reuse in §5.4 and Ch 7:** *image-quality metrics **track the photometric part of the pipeline's mechanism and do not exhaust it***; improvement in them is **neither necessary nor sufficient** for a classification gain, and **no causal claim from image quality to diagnostic performance is made anywhere in the dissertation.** The "better for the eye ≠ better for the CNN" thesis holds in its **weak** form.

**Setup for §4.4 (H-3, Domain Distance Reduction — NEW SECTION):**
- §4.4 now inherits **two explicit debts**: the mechanism conjecture from §4.2.2 and the localization-without-explanation gap from this section. It is positioned as the answer to both.
- **§4.4.1 — measurement protocol:** MMD over penultimate-layer features (representational level) and KL divergence over per-channel histograms (pixel level), source domain EyePACS against six target domains. ⚠️ **NEW-2 is live:** the MMD kernel, per-domain sample size and bootstrap iteration count are **not recorded**; either recover them from the experiment configuration or flag them inline — **do not invent them.**
- **§4.4.2 — results:** MMD falls on **6/6** domains, Δd +0.0700…+0.0931, **all six 95 % CIs exclude zero**; KL falls **34–38 %** on all six.
- **Three interpretive points to carry:** (i) the KL reduction sits in a **narrow band regardless of initial remoteness** → the pipeline compresses photometric spread by a roughly fixed proportion rather than pulling distant domains up to near ones; (ii) the **domain ranking is preserved** (RFMiD most distant, Messidor-2 closest, before and after) → residual difference is substantive — population composition, imaging protocol — and does not reduce to illumination and contrast; (iii) **Stage 7 normalization uses source-domain statistics** and targets do not recompute their own → the convergence is achieved by Stages 0–6 and is **not covert target-domain adaptation**. Point (iii) is important and must appear.
- **⚠️ Report direction, not magnitude correspondence.** Rank correlation between Δd and the transfer gain over the six domains is only ρ ≈ 0.49; only the RFMiD extreme matches cleanly. **The earlier formulation "the smallest distance reduction corresponds to the smallest gain" is false and must not be written** — the smallest gain is DDR's, at a middling Δd. Carry it as *qualitative consistency* (distance falls everywhere, quality rises everywhere) **without** claiming the size of the reduction predicts the size of the gain. This is an association over six points, not causation.
- **Mandatory caveat:** MMD is computed over penultimate-layer features and therefore **within each arm's own representation space** — what is compared is the relative remoteness of a target domain for each model, not distances in a single shared space. Standard practice, but it must be stated.
