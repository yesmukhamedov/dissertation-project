**Key concepts established:**
- **The claim H-2 actually makes about parameters**, stated before the data: an **interior** local optimum, as opposed to a monotone preference for the strongest available setting. The distinction is argued, not assumed — a monotone response would mean the operator is simply being applied as hard as possible; an interior optimum means a real trade-off has been located.
- **Table 4.13 (FIG-4.9)** inline: joint sweep, **eight clip-factor values (0.5–4.0) × five global-threshold values (0.01–0.05) = forty combinations**, on training folds.
- **Interior optimum in both dimensions.** Clip factor: rise to 2.5 then steady decline (0.8136 → 0.7992 → 0.7838 → 0.7754). Global threshold: pronounced maximum at 0.03, declining both sides at every clip factor. **θ\* = (2.5, 0.03)**, applied stochastically with probability 0.80 (Stage 5 is stochastic at train time).
- **Mechanistic reading of the surface shape** (offered as interpretation): raising the clip factor amplifies small low-contrast structure but eventually amplifies sensor noise and non-lesion texture; the global threshold decides *which* frames warrant equalization at all — too permissive equalizes frames that did not need it, too strict leaves degraded frames untreated.
- **Table 4.14 (per-class grids).** Both classes share the threshold optimum at 0.03 but diverge in clip factor: **θ̂(DR1) = 2.5, θ̂(DR2) = 2.0**. Interpretation, labelled as such: microaneurysm-scale evidence (DR1, §1.1.1) rewards more aggressive local equalization than the larger haemorrhages and exudates of DR2. Consequence: **a single global operating point is a compromise across grades** — that is what θ\* is.
- **Held-out confirmation:** CLAHE off 0.7538 → θ\* **0.8137**, Δ **+0.0599**, CI **[+0.0388, +0.0770]**. F1(DR1) 0.0976 → 0.2091; F1(DR2) 0.5316 → 0.6477.
- **The grid/held-out status rule, fixed chapter-wide:** grids are a **selection surface** computed where the model was fitted; **no grid cell is ever reported as an achieved result**; every performance figure attributed to a stage is a held-out figure.
- **The DR1 discrepancy disclosed, not smoothed:** held-out F1(DR1) 0.2091 against grid 0.4693 at the same setting, while DR2 moved the *other* way (0.6477 held out vs 0.5968 grid). Recorded as an open observation whose origin the available evidence does not explain — with the explicit note that the sweep's conclusion rests on the **shape** of the surface and the **held-out difference with its CI**, neither of which depends on a grid cell's absolute level.
