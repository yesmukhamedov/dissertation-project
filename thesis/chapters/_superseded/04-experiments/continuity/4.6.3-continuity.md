Unchanged pairs are largely the §4.6.2 floor-effect cases plus images where attention was already near-coincident.
- **The licensing argument, made before the numbers:** a mean can conceal a modest gain on most images or a large gain on a minority alongside degradation elsewhere, and only the first supports a general statement. The data give the first — *"a property of the typical image rather than of a favourable subset, which is what licenses describing the integrated configuration's attention as better aligned in general rather than better aligned on average."*
- **The reversals are reported and deliberately left uninterpreted.** 4–8 images per type is too few for a subgroup analysis, and *"identifying a pattern in such a group post hoc would be a form of over-fitting to the sample."* Honest summary: consistent for ~7 in 10, reverses for ~1 in 10, reasons unestablished.
- **LIMIT 1 — cross-corpus.** All measurement is on IDRiD, the only available corpus with pixel-level annotation at the required resolution. **Key argument, reusable:** *"performance transfer and attention transfer are distinct properties, and evidence for the former is not evidence for the latter."* Attention-alignment claims are bounded to the IDRiD mask set. This forecloses precisely the inference the original section title invited.
- **LIMIT 2 — the G-3 gap, stated as an ABSENCE in its own paragraph.** H-5 contemplates a qualitative examination on the Kazakh clinical corpus — the corpus closest to the deployment context (§4.1.1, §6.4.2). **The overlays were not produced and no qualitative analysis is reported.** *"This is stated as an absence, not as a negative result: nothing was measured on the clinical corpus and nothing is inferred about it."* Four consequences recorded: (i) H-5's support rests on a single annotated public corpus; (ii) Appendix E is limited to the IDRiD overlays; (iii) closing the gap requires **no additional training** — both arms are fitted and the corpus is available — only overlay generation and expert review; (iv) it is carried to §5.4 as an outstanding item.
- **Bounded closing:** better alignment, 4/4 types, threshold-robust, ~7 images in 10, largest relative gain on the least conspicuous lesion class — *"a statement about alignment between model evidence and clinician annotation"*, explicitly **not** localization, clinical utility, diagnostic reliability or workflow readiness.
