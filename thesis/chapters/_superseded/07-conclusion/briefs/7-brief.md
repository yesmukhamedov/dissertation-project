### THE TWO CEILINGS

Chapter 7 is bounded on both sides and may exceed neither.

- **Behind it, §5.C.** What the evidence supports: a controlled comparison between two configurations under
  identical conditions, replicated across architectures, decomposed across stages, accompanied by a measured
  reduction in distributional distance, and consistent on every corpus and camera grouping examined in both
  training regimes. What it does not support: clinical validity, deployment readiness, device certification,
  lesion localisation, superiority over any published system, extension beyond the corpora and hardware used.
- **In front of it, §0.8.** Chapter 7 may not defend a proposition §0.8 does not submit, at a strength §0.8
  does not assign. **If the two ever disagree, one is wrong and it must be resolved, not averaged.**

### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Section objective:** Close the dissertation on a position a reader can hold against the record.

**Argumentative spine — six movements:**

1. **What the work set out to do, and the thesis it now stands on.** IT-1 restated in one paragraph:
   preprocessing as a component of the model specification rather than preparation of the data, placed
   under controlled contrast. Say plainly what kind of result this is — methodological, about how models of
   this class are specified and compared.
2. **The seven hypothesis outcomes, each in one or two sentences at its established strength, each with its
   fence.** H-1 composite configuration, decomposable not dissolved · H-2 both parts, grouping resolution
   only · H-3 direction only, magnitude does not track gain · H-4 threshold cleared by both arms · H-5
   quantitative half only, alignment not localisation · H-6 dispersion reduction as the substantive content,
   two groupings not independent · H-7 absolute performance not resistance, thin second margin. Plus the
   small-data result as **comparable, not larger**.
3. **What the results amount to jointly — and what they do not.** The principal finding is **consistency,
   not effect size**: present in-domain, decomposable across stages, traceable to a measured reduction in
   distributional distance, observable on every corpus and camera grouping in both training regimes. Then,
   in the same movement, what that consistency does not license.
4. **Contributions**, in four kinds, each already submitted in §0.8 or §0.2 and none newly asserted:
   conceptual (the P1 → P2 reframing); engineering (the pipeline as a specified, reproducible object and its
   five constituent elements); metrological (the asymmetric attention–annotation measure; the direct
   measurement of domain distance with its source-statistics condition); methodological (the defect shared
   by three transfer measures — **strictly descriptive, rehabilitating nothing**). **Include the recorded
   negative result** — the initialisation branch that failed its acceptance gate (VCR-3): a conclusion that
   omits it would misrepresent the record.
5. **Limitations, by shape rather than by enumeration.** Five kinds in a few sentences, with §5.4 named as
   the place they are set out in full and none softened in the compression.
6. **Future work, drawn from §5.C's three groups and kept sorted by what closing each would require** —
   four questions closable without training any new model; three needing new experiments or new data; and
   **one that measurement cannot close at all**. Future work is a programme, not a promise.

**Close:** one short paragraph stating the position the dissertation ends on — what it supports and what it
does not — in a form a reader can check against the record.

**Required tables/figures:** none.
