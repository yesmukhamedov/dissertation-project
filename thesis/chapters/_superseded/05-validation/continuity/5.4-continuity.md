# CONTINUITY NOTE — §5.4 Limitations and Boundary Conditions

**Version:** 7.0.0 · Hands off to §5.C.

---

**Key concepts established:**
- **Register fixed at the outset:** *"a limitation is a boundary on a claim rather than a confession"*, and the purpose of the assembly is to let a reader check that **no claim elsewhere exceeds these bounds**. No item is softened; the single recovery paragraph is the last.
- **Five kinds of boundary**, used as the section's structure: evidence structure, measurements not made, generalization, interpretation, reproducibility and comparison — plus the design chapter.
- **The normalization defect, developed as a secondary methodological contribution.** Three instances of one fault (generalization ratio §4.5.1; retired degradation form §4.7; retention ratio §4.8): each normalizes or differences external performance against the arm's **own** in-domain performance, so a configuration is charged for its in-domain strength. **The identity given in full:** Δ_drop(D) − Δ_drop(C) ≡ Δ_in-domain − Δ_external(X) — a criterion of *less degradation* demands the integrated arm beat the baseline **by more abroad than at home**. Recommendation: such a measure is one of *relative preservation* and **cannot serve as a criterion of external quality unless paired with the absolute external quantity**. **Fence held:** it rehabilitates nothing — H-7 was adjudicated on absolute performance and the degradation form is reported nowhere as evidence.
- **Evidence-structure limits:** single fitted model per arm outside the factorial → intervals narrower than total uncertainty; multiplicity correction not spanning the seven hypotheses; small samples (54 / 26 / 60); one annotated corpus for attention; grouping-resolution-only stage hierarchy; the unexplained selection-versus-held-out divergence at the earliest grade.
- **Seven measurements not made**, each with its consequence: clinical overlays (G-3); cross-corpus attention consistency; mask-channel isolation; photometric parameter interaction; per-grouping confusion matrices; three domain-distance protocol parameters; preprocessing cost.
- **Full register enumeration achieved without list-dumping** — every SB-1.x, SB-2.x, SB-3.x, SB-4.x, DGL-1…6 and the substantive non-claims (NC-3, NC-10…NC-17) appear attached to the statement each bounds.
- **NC-13 stated explicitly:** the candidate's prior publications share authors, data and setup and are **not independent confirmation**.
- **The comparison boundary:** no controlled comparison with any published system; the paradigm representative is not an experimental control; §5.3.1's four-axis incommensurability prevents positioning against deployed systems at all.
- **What survives, stated once at established strength:** identical conditions, two architectures, five-fold patient-level cross-validation, gain on every primary metric; decomposition across stages under a single initialization; accompanying distance reduction on every target domain; the advantage on every external corpus, every camera grouping and both training regimes; better attention alignment on the one corpus where it could be measured.

**Terms introduced:** boundary condition; relative preservation (of ratio-based transfer measures).
