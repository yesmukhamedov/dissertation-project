# CONTINUITY NOTE — §5.2.1 Bootstrap Confidence Intervals and Mixed-Effects Model

**Version:** 7.0.0 · Hands off to §5.2.2.

---

**Key concepts established:**
- **The three sources of variability** — training variability across refits; evaluation-corpus sampling at fixed models; paired case-level discordance. Non-interchangeable; each admits a different procedure and licenses a different kind of statement. *"a design supports a given procedure only if it produced the corresponding replication."* This taxonomy organizes the whole section and is dissertation-original.
- **Table 5.2 — inference procedure by experiment.** The load-bearing reading: **only Experiment 1 admits all three.** Every external evaluation (Experiments 3–6) used one fitted model per arm.
- **Table 5.3 (TAB-5.1)** — the Experiment 1 suite reproduced once as the anchor: bootstrap intervals with gaps ≈ 0.058 / 0.055, DeLong z = 2.87 / 2.99 (p = 0.0041 / 0.0028), McNemar χ² = 7.63 / 8.23 (p = 0.0057 / 0.0041), Holm p = 0.0082 / 0.0056, interaction p = 0.31.
- **Paired tests are primary; marginal-interval separation is weaker** and is stated as such — non-overlap implies significance, the converse does not hold, and separation discards the pairing the shared split makes available.
- **The mixed-effects non-rejection given its exact content:** *"absence of evidence that the arm effect differs across the two architectures; it is not evidence that the effect is identical in them."* Limited power at two architectures. **EH-4 is met as specified in §3.4.2 — the refinement bounds the statement, it does not withdraw it.** §5.2.2 must not upgrade this to demonstrated equivalence.
- **Multiplicity scope stated plainly:** Holm within Experiment 1 across four configurations; **no family-wise correction spans the seven hypotheses.** Defended (hypotheses pre-specified, distinct criteria, distinct corpora, not exchangeable) *and* conceded (**no controlled error rate is claimed over the evidence base as a whole**).
- **The understatement argument** — for Experiments 3–6 the intervals quantify evaluation-corpus sampling only; training variability, the larger component wherever measured here, is absent. **The direction of the bias is known, its magnitude is not.** Correct reading: *"differences between the fitted models on the corpora examined, not estimates of the difference to be expected from a repetition of the whole procedure."*
- **Two residual audit items:** the Experiment 2 dispersion criterion is a heuristic, not a test (§4.3.1); three domain-distance protocol parameters are unrecoverable (§4.4.1), so those intervals are readable but not independently reproducible without re-specification.

**Terms introduced:** between-fold variability; per-instance bootstrap; paired discordance; family-wise correction; random effect (all used in their standard senses; none new to the glossary).

**Argument thread:** The apparatus has been described and audited rather than re-run. What passes forward is a conditioned licence: the in-domain result rests on genuine replication, pre-specified criteria, paired tests and multiplicity correction; the external results rest on fixed models and understated intervals. Claim strengths must be assigned under exactly those conditions.

**Final topic:** What the statistical basis supports within its conditions, and the announcement that §5.2.2 assigns strengths under it plus the §5.1 and Chapter-4 ceilings.
