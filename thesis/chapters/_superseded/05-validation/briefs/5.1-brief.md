### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Section objective:** Establish what the attention evidence adds to the dissertation's case beyond the classification evidence, on what that addition depends, and what ceiling NC-14 and the corpus place on it.

**Argumentative spine:**
- **Thesis:** The attention measurements function as a *corroboration channel* for the classification results rather than as a second measurement of the same quantity — they raise confidence in the interpretation of the classification gain without adding to its magnitude — and the strength of that corroboration is capped by NC-14, by a single annotated corpus, by a single fold, and by the absence of the clinical overlays.
- **Chain:**
  1. Consolidate the §4.6 outcome in one table (Table 5.1: ALO and IoU deltas per lesion type with CIs and p) — **consolidation, not re-derivation**; no new statistic, no re-adjudication.
  2. State the *validation* question §5.1 exists to answer, which §4.6 did not ask: whether the discriminative and the localizational evidence are mutually corroborating, and what that buys.
  3. **The convergence argument (dissertation-original, label it):** a classification-only gain is compatible with a model exploiting corpus-specific regularities outside lesion regions; a simultaneous shift of attention *toward* expert-annotated lesion regions is not what that alternative predicts. The coherence of the two channels therefore bears on the *interpretation* of the gain, not on its size.
  4. **Immediately bound the argument at equal weight** — three limits: (i) the two channels were measured on **different models** (EfficientNet-B4, one fold, a separate training configuration) so the corroboration is directional only, and the +2.2 pp classification margin of the attention arms **must not be set against the +6.55 pp of §4.2.3**; (ii) alignment is not localization (NC-14); (iii) one annotated corpus.
  5. **FIG-5.1** — the per-class overlay gallery is an illustration carrying no measurement; say so, and route the gallery to Appendix E.
  6. **The two absences, in their own paragraph:** the outline's cross-dataset attention-consistency measurement **was not made** (§4.6.3 forecloses inferring it from transfer performance), and the clinical overlays **were not produced** (G-3). Neither is a negative result.
  7. **Hand-off to §5.2.2:** PC-7 may be carried at its established strength *within* NC-14 — the strength refers to the reliability of the alignment finding, not to its reach.

**Required content elements:** the consolidated table and its reading; the validation question; the convergence argument labelled as interpretation; the three bounds; the non-commensurability of the two effect magnitudes; FIG-5.1 as illustration; the two absences; the strength ceiling handed to §5.2.2.
