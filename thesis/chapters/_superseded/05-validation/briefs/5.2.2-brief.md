### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Section objective:** Assign each primary claim its final strength by checking the promotion condition recorded for it in advance, and state the qualification that travels with each label.

**Argumentative spine:**
- **Thesis:** Every empirical primary claim entered the experimental programme at CONDITIONAL with a promotion condition fixed in advance; each of those conditions was met as written, so the uniform outcome is the result of narrow specification rather than lenient scoring — and the labels carry their qualifications with them.
- **Chain:**
  1. State the classification scheme and, decisively, that **the promotion conditions pre-date the evidence** — this is what makes the exercise a check rather than a judgement.
  2. Table 5.4 — for each claim: pre-specified promotion condition, outcome, final strength, qualification carried.
  3. **Confront the inflation objection head-on.** Seven STRONG, none MODERATE, none refuted *is* the pattern that should invite suspicion. Answer in three parts: conditions were fixed in advance and are checkable; the classification is of the claim **as stated**, and the claims were stated narrowly — the discriminating work happened at specification time; and the criteria demonstrably could have failed.
  4. **Show them discriminating.** PC-8 was promotable only because the stage contributions separated by roughly three times the fold-level dispersion; a flat hierarchy would have left it below promotion. The secondary observations — the regularization reading and the calibration improvement — were **not** promoted and remain MODERATE. PC-0 is **not** promoted by any empirical result.
  5. **The qualifications that travel:** PC-7 within NC-14, one corpus, one fold, qualitative half unclosed (G-3); PC-8 at grouping resolution only (NC-17); PC-10 with a margin over the MCID of 0.0041 and a lower confidence bound **below** the MCID — permitted by the criterion as specified, and said so rather than left for the reader to check; PC-6 and PC-9 clear thresholds that **both arms** clear, so the evidence lies in the comparison and the spread reduction.
  6. **CFC-2.8 in its current form:** decomposable, not lifted — the cumulative ablation under a single initialization reproduces the whole in-domain gain, but the integrated arms remain differently initialized, so PC-1's strength attaches to the configuration.
  7. **What STRONG means and does not:** confirmed as stated under the conditions tested; not evidence of generalization, and constrained by the interval understatement of §5.2.1.
  8. Hand-off to §5.3.

**Required tables:** Table 5.4 (TAB-5.2) — final claim-strength classification with pre-specified condition, outcome and qualification.

### COUNTER-ARGUMENT & INHERITED LIMITATIONS

- **Counter-position:** that a table in which every empirical claim reaches the top level is evidence of a lenient scoring procedure rather than of a strong result. This is the section's principal objection and must be answered structurally — by the pre-specification, by the narrowness of what each claim says, and by exhibiting criteria that could have failed and observations that were not promoted.
- **Inherited limitations:** every fence from Chapter 4 and §5.1–§5.2.1 travels with the labels; none is relaxed at classification.
