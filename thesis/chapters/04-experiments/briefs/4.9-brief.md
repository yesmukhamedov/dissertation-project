### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Objective:** Report the small-data result, and extract from it the finding that runs against the experiment's own motivating intuition — the gain is *comparable to*, not larger than, the abundant-data gain.

**Argumentative spine:**
- **Thesis:** The pipeline's advantage survives a training regime two orders of magnitude smaller; and because that advantage is of *similar* magnitude to the abundant-data one rather than larger, it is better understood as a change to the feature space than as a substitute for data.
- **Chain:**
  1. Motivate: every prior experiment fitted on ~35 000 images; a regional clinic has hundreds. Whether the advantage survives, grows or disappears is a distinct question.
  2. State **preregistration** at the outset — design, metrics and analysis fixed before the data were examined.
  3. Design: fit on IDRiD (n = 516) under 5-fold CV, both arms from the same initialization under Table 4.2; evaluate on a held-out Kazakh clinical corpus (n = 60) unused in fitting. IDRiD ≈ 1/70 the size of EyePACS — genuinely scarce, not a subsample.
  4. Render TAB-4.10. Δ wF1 **+0.0798**, κ **+0.1245**, AUC **+0.0482**, accuracy +0.0701. Paired CIs: wF1 [+0.0350, +0.1106], κ [+0.0782, +0.1960], AUC [+0.0183, +0.0707] — all exclude zero. Internal IDRiD CV: 0.5850 → 0.6520, higher in **4 of 5 folds**, single inversion marginal at −0.0057.
  5. **The analytical core — the comparison that runs against intuition.** +0.0798 here vs **+0.0655** on full EyePACS (§4.3.1): comparable, not several times larger. State the natural hypothesis (preprocessing as a substitute for data → advantage should be largest where data are fewest and erode with abundance) and note the data do **not** show that pattern. The similar magnitude is more consistent with the pipeline changing the feature space — a change that persists regardless of corpus size — and this is consistent with §4.4, where the distributional effect was a property of the transform applied identically to every image.
  6. **Limitations, first one most consequential:** n = 60 → the **unpaired** bootstrap intervals **overlap**; separation comes from the **paired** analysis, which is appropriate for identical-data arms and was preregistered. Report the overlap because it correctly indicates the *level* of either arm is imprecisely estimated: what is established is the **difference**, not the level.
  7. Second: 4-of-5 folds is a modest majority — report as a **count**; the inverting fold's identity carries no significance and is not reported.
  8. Third: **SB-2.2** — the clinical corpus is under an institutional data-use agreement; this experiment alone cannot be independently reproduced. Its value is being the only evaluation on deployment-context data, and that value is inseparable from the limitation.
- **Hand-off:** the fifth consequence test is met; §4.C synthesizes.

**Required elements:** preregistration stated; design with the same-initialization note; TAB-4.10 with paired CIs; internal CV as a count; the comparable-not-larger analysis with the declined stronger claim; the three limitations in that order.
