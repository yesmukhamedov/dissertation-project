### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Section objective:** Report the two-dimensional sweep of the dual-constraint CLAHE parameters, establish that the response surface has an interior optimum in both dimensions, show that the per-class optima do not coincide, confirm the selected setting on held-out data, and bound the result by DGL-5.

**Argumentative spine (required):**
- **Thesis:** The contrast-enhancement stage is not a switch but a tuned operator: its response surface is non-monotone in both controlling parameters with a maximum strictly inside the tested range, the optimum differs by lesion scale across classes, and the selected setting survives held-out confirmation — which together establish the parametric half of H-2 while forbidding any claim that the chosen values are universal.
- **Reasoning chain:**
  1. Open from §4.3.1: CLAHE ranked second among stage contributions; a stage that carries 19 % of the gain warrants an account of *how* its parameters govern that contribution.
  2. Recall the two parameters from §3.1.2 — the clip factor and the global threshold of the dual-constraint rule — without re-deriving the formalization.
  3. Render the weighted-F1 grid inline. Read it in both dimensions: along the clip factor, the response rises to 2.5 and then falls (0.8136 → 0.7992 → 0.7838 → 0.7754); along the global threshold, a pronounced maximum at 0.03 with decline on both sides. **The maximum is interior, not on an edge** — which is what PC-2 requires and what distinguishes a tuned operator from one that simply prefers its strongest available setting.
  4. State the selected operating point θ\* = (clip 2.5, threshold 0.03) with the stochastic application probability of 0.80 (§3.1.1, Stage 5 is stochastic at training time).
  5. Render the per-class grids and make the analytical point: F1(DR1) peaks at (2.5, 0.03) while F1(DR2) peaks at (2.0, 0.03). The divergence is in the clip factor, and it is interpretable — the smaller structures that define mild disease (microaneurysms, §1.1.1) reward more aggressive local equalization than the larger haemorrhages and exudates that define moderate disease. Label this as an interpretation consistent with the profile, not as an independently measured mechanism.
  6. Report the held-out confirmation: CLAHE off 0.7538 → θ\* 0.8137, Δ = +0.0599 with 95 % CI [+0.0388, +0.0770]; F1(DR1) 0.0976 → 0.2091; F1(DR2) 0.5316 → 0.6477.
  7. **Report the grid-versus-held-out divergence honestly.** The held-out F1(DR1) at θ\* (0.2091) is far below the grid value at the same point (0.4693), while F1(DR2) is higher (0.6477 against 0.5968). State that the grid values are a *selection surface* computed on training folds, not performance estimates; that the held-out figures are the ones this dissertation cites as performance; and that the size of the DR1 discrepancy is not explained by the available evidence.
  8. Bound it: DGL-5 (values tuned on this corpus, portability not established); CFC-1.2 (no extrapolation beyond the tested ranges); single evaluation per grid point, no fold-wise spread; interaction with the flat-field parameter not investigated (forward to §4.3.3).
- **Conclusion / hand-off:** The CLAHE half of PC-2 is confirmed; §4.3.3 performs the corresponding sweep for the flat-field parameter and relates both to the image-quality metrics.
