### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Objective:** Report the zero-shot transfer of both arms to APTOS 2019, apply the G criterion, and state the verdict together with the two features that qualify it — that **both** arms clear the threshold, and that G's construction makes it a conservative measure of the arm difference.

**Argumentative spine:**
- **Thesis:** The integrated configuration transfers to an unseen public corpus better than the baseline by a substantial and interval-separated margin; but the pre-specified threshold does not by itself discriminate the arms, so the substantive finding is the comparison, not the threshold pass — and saying so is what keeps the claim honest.
- **Chain:**
  1. Open from §4.4.2: distance falls on every domain; §4.5 tests the first consequence — does performance survive a corpus the model never saw?
  2. Protocol: EyePACS-trained models applied to APTOS 2019 (n = 3 662) **without retraining or fine-tuning**; no target-domain statistics used at any stage (§4.4.1). State that zero-shot means exactly this.
  3. Define G (OD-4) and why it is normalized: it asks what *fraction* of in-domain competence survives the domain change, which is comparable across arms with different in-domain levels.
  4. Render TAB-4.6 headline inline: C wF1 0.6465, AUC 0.7940, κ 0.7887, acc 0.6338, macro-F1 0.4649, **G = 0.8577**; D wF1 0.7354, AUC 0.8263, κ 0.8874, acc 0.7272, macro-F1 0.5666, **G = 0.8976**.
  5. Pairwise: ΔwF1 +0.0889 CI [+0.0681, +0.1197]; ΔAUC +0.0323 CI [+0.0224, +0.0482]. Both exclude zero.
  6. **Verdict:** `h4_supported`. G_D = 0.8976 ≥ 0.85 with a margin of 0.048.
  7. **The qualification, stated immediately and not buried:** G_C = 0.8577 also clears 0.85. The threshold therefore does **not** discriminate between the arms; what discriminates them is the comparison. Correct formulation: *"the pipeline does not rescue transfer, it improves transfer that was already acceptable."*
  8. **The G-denominator asymmetry:** G is computed against each arm's own in-domain score, and the integrated arm has the larger denominator (0.8193 vs 0.7538). The ratio gain (+0.040) is therefore **by construction more conservative** than the absolute gain (+0.089). Note this is the same structural defect analysed for the retired H-7 metric (§4.7, §5.4) in a milder form — a normalized measure penalizing an arm for its in-domain strength.
  9. Caveats: **fold-0 evaluation** — no between-fold variance, so the CIs are per-instance bootstrap rather than per-fold; DGL-1; CFC-2.8 (the arms still differ by initialization, though §4.3.1 has shown preprocessing sufficient for an effect of this size in-domain).
- **Hand-off:** §4.5.2 examines *where* the transfer advantage arises — per class, in the error structure, and at the referral threshold.

**Required elements:** protocol with zero-shot defined; G defined and motivated; headline table; pairwise Δ with CIs; verdict; both-arms-clear qualification; G-denominator asymmetry; fold-0 and DGL-1 caveats.
