### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Section objective:** Report the flat-field σ sweep, establish its interior unimodal optimum and held-out confirmation, then use the per-level image-quality measurements to establish the section's more consequential finding — that image-quality metrics track the *photometric* part of the pipeline's mechanism and only that part, so they are not a sufficient proxy for the classification gain.

**Argumentative spine (required):**
- **Thesis:** The flat-field parameter behaves as a tuned operator with a sharp interior optimum that the sweep confirms rather than revises; and the image-quality measurements, read across all eight ablation levels, show that such metrics register only the two photometric stages — about half the total gain — while the geometric and stochastic stages contribute a comparable amount entirely invisibly to them. Image quality is therefore a partial diagnostic of the mechanism, not a substitute for measuring classification.
- **Reasoning chain:**
  1. Open from §4.3.2: the CLAHE half of PC-2 is closed; the flat-field stage — ranked *first* in §4.3.1 at 22 % of the gain — is the remaining parametric question.
  2. Recall the parameter from §3.1.1: σ expressed as a fraction of the field-of-view diameter, so that the correction scales with frame geometry rather than pixel count.
  3. Render the σ grid inline (σ/D_FOV against weighted F1 and CNR). Read it: strictly unimodal, maximum at 0.07, symmetric decline both sides (0.7662 ← 0.8089 → 0.7577). Range across the sweep R = 0.0512 — **comparable to the full pipeline effect of +0.0655**, so the parameter is genuinely consequential and not a detail.
  4. Note that σ\* = 0.07·D coincides with the value already fixed in the pipeline specification (§3.1.1) — the sweep **confirms** the adopted setting rather than changing it. Say this plainly; it is a validation result, not a tuning result.
  5. Held-out confirmation: flat-field off 0.7513 → σ\* 0.8087, Δ = +0.0574, CI [+0.0428, +0.0806].
  6. Note the *within-stage* CNR agreement: the CNR maximum (3.93) falls exactly at σ\*, and the CNR ranking matches the weighted-F1 ranking at all six points. Set this up as the contrast for what follows.
  7. Render **TAB-4.5** (CNR, entropy, SSIM per ablation level, with wF1 alongside) and read it level by level:
     - Geometric stages L1–L3: CNR 20.43 → 20.38 and entropy 5.502 → 5.514 (within rounding); only SSIM falls, reflecting geometric transformation. Yet wF1 rises +2.21 pp. **The gain is invisible to the metrics.**
     - L4 flat-field: the only stage that raises CNR substantially (20.38 → 28.60, +40 %) — and the largest single classification contributor.
     - L5 CLAHE: *lowers* CNR (28.60 → 24.15) while producing the largest entropy jump (5.596 → 5.884) — local equalization buys detail at the cost of global lesion-against-background contrast. Second-largest contributor.
     - L6 augmentation: metrics literally unchanged (train-time-only stage, quality measured on the validation configuration), yet +0.0101 wF1.
     - SSIM falls monotonically 1.000 → 0.865 while wF1 rises monotonically — distance from the original frame is not degradation.
  8. **The synthesis:** the two levels that move the metrics are exactly the two largest contributors (41 % of the gain), so the metrics are not noise — they flag the leading part of the mechanism. But 49 % of the gain occurs at levels where the metrics do not move at all. Correct formulation: *image-quality metrics track the photometric part of the mechanism and do not exhaust it.* The thesis "better for the eye is not the same as better for the CNN" holds in its weak form — such metrics are not a sufficient predictor of classification gain.
  9. Bounds: DGL-5; CFC-1.2 (range 0.05–0.10 only, step 0.01, no finer grid); single evaluation per point; **CNR values in the two tables use different normalizations and must not be compared across tables** (SIR-3); n = 100 for the image-quality sample, no spread; Stage 6 invisible by construction; **VVI not reported — not implemented**.
- **Conclusion / hand-off:** PC-2 is closed on both parameters; H-2 is established in both its component and parametric arms. §4.4 asks what the photometric stages are doing that the classification metrics register and the image-quality metrics only partly capture — by measuring domain distance directly.
