### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Section objective:** Specify the restored 2×2 factorial design of Experiment 1 — the two factors, the four configurations, the initialization source of each arm, the evaluation corpus and partitioning, and the pre-committed EH-3/EH-4 acceptance criterion — and state precisely, in advance of the results, what the design can and cannot establish.

**Argumentative spine (required):**
- **Thesis:** Experiment 1 is constructed so that the contrast it measures is a contrast between two *paradigms* under matched conditions, not a comparison of two implementations: everything that is not the manipulated factor — corpus, partitioning, optimizer, schedule, loss, stopping rule, evaluation metric suite — is held identical across all four cells, and the acceptance criterion is fixed before the data are seen.
- **Reasoning chain:**
  1. Open from §4.1.3: the data substrate and the execution environment are fixed; what remains is the design that turns them into a test of H-1.
  2. State the two factors — *preprocessing arm* (baseline 3-channel vs. full 8-stage pipeline 4-channel) × *backbone* (ResNet-50 vs. EfficientNet-B3) — and enumerate the four resulting configurations A, B, C, D.
  3. Give the paradigmatic reading (Task 2.6.1/2.6.2 of the chapter README, verbatim commitments): A/C instantiate P1 as an operational construct under OD-3; B/D operationalise P2. Mark the forbidden phrasings as out of bounds.
  4. State the initialization asymmetry openly: the baseline arm initializes from ImageNet, the integrated arm from a gate-selected in-domain initialization, so the manipulated variable in Experiment 1 is **composite** — *(preprocessing, initialization source)* — and CFC-2.8 forbids attributing the outcome to preprocessing alone **within this experiment**. Immediately signpost §4.3: a cumulative ablation on the same corpus under a *single* initialization measures the preprocessing contribution separately, so the composite is decomposed elsewhere in the chapter rather than left standing as an inferential ceiling.
  5. State the evaluation protocol: EyePACS at 100 % (n = 35 126), 5-fold patient-level grade-stratified cross-validation, the four primary metrics in their governance priority order, the shared training configuration of Table 4.2 (§4.1.3).
  6. State the acceptance rule *before* results: EH-3 conjunctively, EH-4 as the replication requirement, and note that the 2×2 form is what makes EH-4 testable within a single experiment rather than across experiments.
  7. Bound the design: NC-6 (the two backbones are chosen as representatives of two distinct design families, not as optima), DGL-1/DGL-2, and the observation that a factorial with a composite factor licenses a claim about the *integrated configuration*, which is exactly the claim H-1 makes.
- **Conclusion / hand-off:** The design is fixed and its acceptance criterion pre-committed; §4.2.2 reports how the four configurations behaved during fitting, and §4.2.3 evaluates them against EH-3/EH-4.
