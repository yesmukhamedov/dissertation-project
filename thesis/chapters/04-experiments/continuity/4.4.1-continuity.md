# CONTINUITY NOTE — §4.4.1 Measurement Protocol: MMD over Representations and KL over Channel Histograms

**Version:** 7.0.0 · PART 2 of the §4.4.1 session · **Opens §4.4 (H-3, NEW SECTION). Protocol only — no measurement value reported.**

---

**Key concepts established:**
- **The gap §4.4 exists to fill, stated explicitly:** §4.2.2 inferred the mechanism, §4.3.1 localized it, §4.3.3 showed image-quality metrics capture only part of it — *"The mechanism has been repeatedly inferred and never measured."* H-3 states it directly and is testable **without reference to any classification outcome**.
- **H-3 stated**, and now **formally defined in governance v7.1.0** (HYPOTHESIS §H-3; PC-11 in ARGUMENT_MAP; SC-I in CONTRIBUTIONS): the pipeline reduces distributional distance between source (EyePACS) and target domains.
- **THE ACCEPTANCE CRITERION — a *K*-of-*n* rule, K = 5, n = 6**, stated in the body before any measurement:
  `H-3 ⟺ Σ_X PASS_S(d, X) ≥ 5`, with `PASS_S(d, X) ⟺ Δd(X) = d(BASE,X) − d(INT,X) ≥ MCID_d = 0.0 ∧ CI⁻(Δd) > 0`. Because `d` is unnormalized, **MCID_d = 0.0** and the per-corpus test degenerates to directional significance. CIs from **1 000 bootstrap resamples**. Two properties flagged for the reader: the rule **tolerates one dissenting corpus**, and the sign is already normalized by defining Δd as a *reduction*, so the ordinary superiority form applies.
- **Threshold provenance disclosed:** neither MCID_d nor K was fixed when the question was posed; both are assigned at formalization, and the sensitivity of the verdict to that assignment is handed to §4.4.2.
- **Arms and cost:** integrated − baseline on **EfficientNet-B3** (configurations D − C of Experiment 1); **no training of any kind** — both models are already fitted and each corpus is passed forward once through each.
- **METRIC ROLES ARE ASYMMETRIC AND THE ASYMMETRY IS DELIBERATE.** **MMD over penultimate-layer features is PRIMARY and the criterion is computed on it alone.** **KL over channel histograms is SECONDARY and INFORMATIONAL ONLY — no part of the criterion rests on it.** Rationale stated frankly: a pipeline containing illumination correction and contrast equalization is *expected* to converge intensity histograms, so admitting the pixel-level measure to the criterion *"would allow the hypothesis to be satisfied by something it does not assert."* It is reported for **where** the convergence originates, not as evidence that convergence matters.
- **The two levels can genuinely disagree**, and the criterion records that: a pixel-level reduction with no representational reduction would be a **negative** result for H-3 despite the histograms converging.
- **Six target domains:** APTOS 2019, IDRiD, Messidor-2, DDR, ODIR-5K, RFMiD — spanning **all three non-training tiers** of §4.1.1 (external, clinical, device), so the measurement covers population, protocol and camera-hardware shift together.
- **Two measures, two levels, and the argument for needing both:** KL over per-channel intensity histograms measures where the transform *acts* — and its near-tautology risk is **conceded openly** (*"a pixel-level reduction alone would be close to a restatement of what the stages do"*). MMD over penultimate-layer features measures the space the classifier *consumes*, and is **not entailed** by the pixel result: a transform can homogenize intensities while leaving representations as far apart as before, if what separates domains is structural rather than photometric. **The representational measure carries the weight of the hypothesis.**
- **MANDATORY CAVEAT, stated at the protocol not deferred:** MMD is computed over model-dependent features, so baseline and integrated distances live in **different feature spaces**. What is compared is *the relative remoteness of a target domain within each arm's own representation*, not two distances in one shared metric space. KL carries no such qualification (model-independent).
- **THE DESIGN PROPERTY THAT FORECLOSES THE DECISIVE OBJECTION:** Stage 7 normalizes with **source-domain statistics**; target domains never recompute their own. Convergence is therefore achieved by **Stages 0–6**, applied identically everywhere, with **no information about the target distribution entering the transform at any point** — it is not covert target-domain adaptation. This sentence must survive into §4.4.2, §4.C and Chapter 7.
- **PRE-SPECIFIED REVERSAL CASE, on the record before the outcome.** Stage 5 (CLAHE tuned on the source corpus) and Stage 7 (source-domain statistics) are **source-bound by construction**, so it was a live possibility that the pipeline would reduce variability *within* the source domain while *increasing* it across domains. Such an outcome *"would have been an established finding rather than a failed measurement: it would have accounted directly for any reversal observed in the external evaluations of §4.5 and §4.7."* This is what makes H-3 genuinely falsifiable rather than a formality, and it is retained in governance v7.1.0.
- **Two `[VERIFY]` flags remain, no values invented:** MMD kernel and bandwidth; per-domain sample size. **The bootstrap iteration count is now resolved — 1 000 resamples** (governance v7.1.0). Consequence stated: direction and interval separation do not depend on the two open parameters, **full reproducibility of interval widths does**.

**Terms introduced:** maximum mean discrepancy (MMD); Kullback–Leibler divergence; penultimate-layer features; source domain; target domain; distributional convergence.

**Argument thread:** The chapter turns from *what* and *where* to *why*. The protocol is built so that the answer cannot be manufactured — two levels that can disagree, and a transform that never sees the target.

**Final topic:** The three unrecorded parameters, flagged rather than filled in, and the hand-off to §4.4.2.
