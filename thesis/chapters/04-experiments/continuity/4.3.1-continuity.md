**Key concepts established:**
- **The design premise that licenses everything else:** eight cumulative levels, stages added in pipeline order, and **the same initialization weights at every level**. Corpus, folds, backbone (EfficientNet-B3), optimizer, schedule, loss and stopping rule all identical to Experiment 1. With initialization fixed, preprocessing content is the only varying quantity.
- **Δⱼ is order-conditional**, stated twice: it is the contribution of stage *j* **given** stages 0…j−1, not an isolated effect. Interactions are unmeasured.
- **Table 4.11 (TAB-4.4)** inline: L0 0.7538 → L7 0.8193; cumulative ΔwF1 **+0.0655**, ΔAUC +0.0360, Δκ +0.1103, ΔAcc +0.0779.
- **THE DECOMPOSITION.** L0 and L7 coincide numerically with configurations C and D (0.7538 / 0.8193), and +0.0655 matches Experiment 1's +6.55 pp. **Phrasing fixed and to be reused verbatim in §4.C, Ch 5 and Ch 7:** *"because the initialization was held constant at every level, the cumulative addition of the eight preprocessing stages reproduces, on its own, the entire performance difference that Experiment 1 measured … The preprocessing contribution is therefore separable from the initialization contribution."* **With the fence:** Experiment 1 is **not** retroactively converted into a single-factor manipulation; its verdict remains a verdict about the integrated configuration as a unitary system. The two statements are compatible and neither substitutes for the other.
- **Two anti-coincidence arguments:** (i) **per-fold monotonicity** — L0 < L1 < … < L7 in all five folds with no inversion anywhere, far stronger than monotonicity of means; (ii) **metric-ratio consistency** — κ rises most, AUC least, the same ordering as Experiment 1, with the same distant-error explanation.
- **7/7 significance**, |Δⱼ| ∈ [0.0065, 0.0143] against noise bands [0.0042, 0.0060] → no stage redundant. **The 2·σ_fold rule is named as a heuristic, explicitly weaker than the paired tests of §4.2.3/§5.2.1.**
- **Table 4.12 (ranking).** Flat-field 0.0143 (22 %), CLAHE 0.0125 (19 %) lead — **41 % combined**, ≈ as much as the four geometric/normalization stages together. **Resolution rule adopted chapter-wide:** the data resolve the **grouping** (photometric ≫ rest), not a strict 1-to-7 order; the rank-6/rank-7 gap (0.0068 vs 0.0065) is inside the noise band. *"Claims in this dissertation that rest on the hierarchy are made at the grouping resolution only."*
- **Three limitations in the body:** fixed order → interactions unmeasured and increments redistributable under a different order; **Stage 3 not isolated** (L3 = Stages 2+3 jointly; rank 4 belongs to the pair, mask contribution unmeasured); NC-17 — the stage set is not asserted optimal or complete.

**Terms introduced:** cumulative ablation; ablation level; order-conditional contribution; between-fold standard deviation (σ_fold); noise band; grouping resolution.
