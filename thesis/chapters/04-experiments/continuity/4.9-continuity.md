**Key concepts established:**
- **Preregistration stated at the outset:** design, metrics and analysis fixed before the data were examined — the only experiment in the chapter for which this holds.
- **Design:** fit on **IDRiD (n = 516)** under 5-fold CV, **both arms from the same initialization** under Table 4.2; evaluate on a held-out **Kazakh clinical corpus (n = 60)** that played no part in fitting. IDRiD ≈ **1/70** the size of EyePACS — *"genuinely data-scarce rather than a subsampled approximation of an abundant one."* Backbone EfficientNet-B3.
- **Note on CFC-2.8:** because both arms share an initialization here, the composite-factor caveat does **not** apply in its Experiment-1 form. This is stated in the design paragraph, and no claim beyond what the design supports is made.
- **Table 4.28 (TAB-4.10)** inline: wF1 0.5134 ± 0.0450 → **0.5932 ± 0.0400** (Δ **+0.0798**); AUC 0.7417 → **0.7899** (+0.0482); κ 0.4876 → **0.6121** (+0.1245); accuracy +0.0701. Paired 95 % CIs all exclude zero: wF1 [+0.0350, +0.1106], κ [+0.0782, +0.1960], AUC [+0.0183, +0.0707]. Internal IDRiD CV 0.5850 ± 0.0380 → 0.6520 ± 0.0310, higher in **4 of 5 folds**, single inversion marginal (−0.0057).
- **THE ANALYTICAL CORE — a finding that runs against the experiment's own motivating intuition.** The scarce-regime gain **+0.0798** is **comparable to** the abundant-data gain **+0.0655** (§4.3.1) — *not* several times larger. The natural hypothesis (preprocessing as a substitute for data: normalize away nuisance variation a network would otherwise need many examples to learn to ignore → advantage largest where examples are fewest, eroding with abundance) is **stated and then shown not to hold**. The similar magnitude is *"more consistent with the pipeline changing the feature space the classifier operates in — a change that persists regardless of how much data is available to exploit it — than with its compensating for missing examples"*, and consistent with §4.4, where the distributional effect was a property of the transform applied identically to every image irrespective of corpus size. **Reusable in §4.C and Chapter 7 at exactly this strength.**
- **Three limitations, most consequential first:**
  1. **n = 60 → the *unpaired* bootstrap intervals OVERLAP.** Separation comes from the **paired** analysis, which is appropriate for identical-data arms and was preregistered. The overlap is reported because it correctly indicates the *level* of either arm is imprecisely estimated: *"What the experiment establishes with reasonable confidence is the difference between the arms, not the level of either."*
  2. **4-of-5 folds is a modest majority** — reported as a **count**, not as uniform superiority; the inverting fold's identity carries no significance and is not reported.
  3. **SB-2.2** — the clinical corpus is held under an institutional data-use agreement, so **this experiment alone among those in the chapter cannot be independently reproduced**. Its value is being the only evaluation on deployment-context data, *"and that value is inseparable from the reproducibility limitation."*
