**Key concepts established:**
- **Table 4.4 (convergence)** rendered inline: per-fold best epochs, train loss, validation loss and **generalization gap** for A/B/C/D. Integrated arms peak at epochs **7–10** vs **14–17** for baseline; gap **0.021–0.022** vs **0.052–0.054** (≈2.5× smaller).
- **The joint-signature argument** (the section's analytical core): the integrated arms carry a **higher** train loss (0.126–0.131 vs 0.098–0.102) at comparable validation loss. Fitting the training corpus *less* closely while generalizing at least as well is inconsistent both with "merely easier to fit" (which shows as *lower* train loss) and with "learns less" (which shows as *higher* val loss). Reading: **reduced effective capacity**, i.e. regularization.

- **Reproducibility of the regime:** within-arm best-epoch spread ≈ ±1–1.5 epochs, **no overlap between arms** on either backbone, under fixed seed and fold assignment → attributable to the arm, not to a seed.
- **Measurement limitation stated up front:** per-epoch trajectories were **not retained**; the analysis rests on per-fold best-epoch summaries. **FIG-4.5 is consequently not cited** — no schematic is presented as a measured curve.
