# CONTINUITY NOTE — §4.6.1 Grad-CAM Generation Protocol

**Version:** 7.0.0 · PART 2 of the §4.6.1 session · **Opens §4.6 (Experiment 4, H-5). Protocol only — no measurement reported.**

---

**Key concepts established:**
- **NC-14 stated FIRST, as a property of the instrument rather than a hedge.** A Grad-CAM map shows where the evidence driving an output is concentrated; it does not show that a lesion was detected, delineated or clinically recognized. A map may concentrate on a region for reasons unrelated to the pathology there, and a region may be diagnostically decisive without dominating the gradient. Overlap with an expert annotation is evidence of **alignment**, nothing stronger. **Binding on §4.6.2, §4.6.3, §5.1 and Appendix E.**
- **Generation:** Grad-CAM (§2.5.1) at the final convolutional block of **EfficientNet-B4**, both arms, same images, upsampled to input resolution, thresholded at τ.
- **Paired design and its rationale:** both arms map every image, so per-image difficulty differences out. *"An unpaired design on a corpus of this size would be dominated by between-image variation and would be uninformative regardless of the effect present."*
- **Corpus, stated without softening:** of IDRiD's 516 images, **54** carry usable pixel-level masks. Per-type counts differ — microaneurysms 54, hard exudates 54, haemorrhages 53, **soft exudates 26**. IDRiD provenance limitations (single-centre, single-camera, no class-distribution statistic) are inherited by every §4.6.2 quantity (SIR-2).
- **ALO primary / IoU secondary, with the diffuseness caveat PRE-stated** so §4.6.2 is not misread: an attention map is not a segmentation, it is diffuse by construction and spreads weight over a receptive field far larger than a microaneurysm, so its union with a small mask is dominated by the attention region. **Low absolute IoU is expected and is not a failure**; IoU is reported for its *change between arms*, not its level.
- **Statistics fixed in advance:** paired one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank per lesion type, bootstrap CIs on the paired difference, τ = 0.5 operating point, **τ sweep over {0.2, 0.3, 0.5, 0.7}** specified as a robustness check rather than a post-hoc rescue.
- **Floor-effect control specified in advance:** if overlap is zero in both arms the pair carries no information, so the zero-in-both proportion must be reported alongside the effect.
- **Fold-0 evaluation** stated.

**Terms introduced:** attention map; attention–lesion overlap (ALO); intersection over union (IoU); paired comparison; Wilcoxon signed-rank; floor effect; attention threshold τ.

**Argument thread:** From performance to inspectability. The chapter narrows its explainability claim deliberately and builds a protocol that makes the narrow claim testable rather than making a broad one plausible.

**Final topic:** The fold-0 limitation and the hand-off to §4.6.2 / §4.6.3.
