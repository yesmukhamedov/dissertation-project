## DIVERGENCE FROM `MASTER_OUTLINE.md` — RECORDED, NOT SILENT

The outline (v6.0.0) gives the object as *"Fundus images of patients with diabetic retinopathy, sourced
from EyePACS…"* — i.e. it names the material as the object and then substitutes a corpus list for a
definition. This draft departs from it on both counts, giving the object as the **process** and naming the
corpus as its material. Grounds: the house convention that an object of research is a process or system,
and `thesis/output/abstract_en.md`, which is the formulation verified against the real IITU samples and
which gives the object as *"The process of automated diagnosis of diabetic retinopathy from colour fundus
images using convolutional neural networks."*

**Consequence:** `outline/MASTER_OUTLINE.md` should be corrected in the governance-sync pass. Until then
this draft and the abstract agree, and the outline is the outlier.
