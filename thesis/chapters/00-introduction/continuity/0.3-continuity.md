**Key concepts established:**
- **The goal sentence proper** (first paragraph), which enumerates the eight stages in canonical OD-3 order,
  names the pipeline as an integral component of the model, and fixes the mode of validation as a
  *controlled contrast* against an equivalent configuration trained without it. This paragraph is quoted in
  the abstract and defence materials and must be preserved verbatim through revision.
- **Three commitments distinguished from the headline sentence:**
  1. *Unit of evaluation is the configuration, not the network* — and with it the matched-conditions rule
     (same corpus, partitioning, budget, hardware, evaluation protocol). Includes the sentence that
     pre-honours CFC-2.8 at goal level: where configurations differ in more than one respect, the claim is
     bounded to the configuration as a whole.
  2. *Five validation directions*, stated as inquiry and not as outcome: in-domain contrast plus stage
     decomposition; direct measurement of source-to-target distance in feature space; transfer without
     retraining; attention–annotation alignment; external clinical corpora, camera groupings and the
     data-scarce regime.
  3. *The computational envelope constrains the goal*, rather than qualifying it after the fact.
- **The explicit disclaimer of optimisation:** *"the goal is accordingly not to obtain the best achievable
  classifier"* — which is what keeps SB-3.1 honest from the first page.
- **The closing boundary:** a methodological result, not clinical validity, regulatory compliance or
  readiness for unsupervised deployment.

canonical form and fix the terminology for every later use.
