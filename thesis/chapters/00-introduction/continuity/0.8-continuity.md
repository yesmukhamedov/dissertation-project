**Key concepts established:**
- **The rule that defines the section, stated in its opening:** a provision carries a qualification that is
  not detachable, and *"a provision stated without it is a different and stronger proposition than the
  evidence supports, and is not the one defended here."*
- **Eleven provisions, grouped by kind** — one conceptual (PC-0), eight empirical (PC-1, PC-2, PC-8, PC-11,
  PC-6, PC-7, PC-9, PC-10), one methodological (the transfer-measure defect), one combining the two
  non-empirical contributions (PC-4, PC-5) — plus the small-data result submitted deliberately **as an
  observation and not as a provision**.
- **The register gap is left open**: PC-3 is not in use, stated in the opening, and the demoted two-stage
  fine-tuning claim is not resurrected to fill it.
- **All eight fences survive compression**, which is the whole test of this section: composite configuration
  with decomposition-not-dissolution (2); grouping resolution only (4); direction only (5); both arms clear
  the threshold (6 and 8); alignment not localisation, one corpus, one fold, qualitative half unevaluated
  (7); two of five camera groupings not independent (8); performance not resistance, with the **0.0041**
  margin and the sub-threshold lower bound disclosed (9); small-data gain comparable, not larger.
- **Exactly one metric value appears in the section**, and it is a fence rather than an achievement. No table
  is reproduced, so the Introduction cannot be used to re-adjudicate Chapter 4.
- **The uniformity of the levels is explained rather than displayed** — a property of how narrowly the claims
  were specified and of conditions fixed in advance, *"not a measure of their importance."*
- **The closing enumeration of what is not submitted** — clinical validity, unsupervised deployment
  readiness, device certification, localisation, superiority over any named system, and extension beyond the
  corpora, devices and hardware used. This is the position ceiling from §5.C, restated at the front of the
  document where a reader meets it first.

**Terms introduced:** none. Every term is at the form fixed in §0.6 or in Chapters 4–5.

**Argument thread:** The Introduction's substantive work is complete — gap, goal, objectives, reference,
hypotheses with their criteria, novelty, and now the defended positions with their fences. What remains is
apparatus: the methodological basis, the two significance sections, reliability, empirical basis,
approbation, programmes, publications, and the structural description. None of them may introduce a claim.

**Final topic:** What is not submitted, and why the levels are uniform.

**Setup for the remaining sections (§0.7, §0.9, §0.10, §0.11, §0.13, §0.14, §0.15, §0.12, §0.16):**
- **None of these sections may state an outcome that §0.8 has not already submitted.** They describe method,
  significance, reliability apparatus, material and provenance — not findings. §0.9 and §0.10 are the two
  most likely to drift, because "significance" invites restating results as importance.
- **§0.7 Methodological Basis** — the methods as a system: controlled experimental comparison under matched
  conditions; the multi-metric framework in its EH-1 order; patient-level stratified cross-validation;
  paired significance testing, interval estimation, cross-fold modelling and correction for multiplicity;
  explainability by attention–annotation overlap; image-quality characterisation. State the two aggregate
  concessions §5.2.1 established — **correction for multiplicity is scoped to one experiment, so no
  dissertation-wide error rate is claimed**, and evaluations resting on a single fitted model understate
  total uncertainty in a known direction. They belong in the methodological basis, not only in Chapter 5.
- **§0.9 Theoretical Significance** — the conceptual reframing, the formalisations (dual-constraint clip
  limit, adaptive flat-field scale, ALO), and the thermal-optical model with SB-1.5 attached. Significance
  of a *theoretical* kind; no empirical outcome restated.
- **§0.10 Practical Significance** — bounded by SB-4.1, SB-4.3 and SIR-8: **no projected national outcome may
  appear as a result of this work**, and the design status of the architecture must be stated in the same
  passage that describes its applicability.
- **§0.13 Reliability of the Results** — the apparatus that makes the results checkable (corpus scale,
  partitioning with leakage control, multi-metric reporting, interval estimation, pre-registered criteria),
  **including the two concessions above**. Reliability sections that list only strengths are not credible.
- **§0.14 Empirical Basis** — the eight corpora by function, with the camera provenance and the annotation
  subsets; SB-2.2 on the non-redistributable clinical corpus; SB-2.3 on taxonomy differences.
- **§0.11 Approbation and §0.12 Publications** — from Appendix D and the publication register; SIR-4 on every
  entry and SIR-5 where two records report one work. The count is **five distinct publications**, since the
  Scopus-indexed article appears under two literature records.
- **§0.15 Connection with Scientific Programmes** — the state programme framing already verified in the
  abstract; a correspondence of research direction, never a claim of funding or of mandate.
- **§0.16 Structure and Length** — written **last**, since it reports the finished document's shape.
