**Section objective:** Let a reader navigate the document and know what each part contains before reaching it.

**Argumentative spine:**
- **The composition:** front matter, an introduction, six chapters, a conclusion, a list of references, and
  six appendices.
- **One clause per chapter**, saying what it establishes rather than what it is titled:
  Ch 1 the problem and the gap · Ch 2 the mathematical and theoretical apparatus · Ch 3 the methodology and
  the evaluation framework · Ch 4 the experimental programme · Ch 5 the reliability validation, the claim

  of references, number of appendices — with the note that these are fixed at final assembly.
- **One sentence on the ordering principle**, so that the structure reads as an argument and not as a
  table of contents in prose: the chapters proceed from the problem to the apparatus to the method to the
  evidence to the price of that evidence to the design it licenses.
