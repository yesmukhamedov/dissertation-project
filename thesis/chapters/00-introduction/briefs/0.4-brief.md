### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Section objective:** Make the dissertation's structure checkable against its own plan — one objective per
chapter, jointly exhausting the goal, with nothing left over on either side.

**Argumentative spine:**
- **Opening (two sentences):** the objectives are a decomposition of §0.3, and the mapping to chapters is
  stated because it is what allows the reader to audit whether the work does what it undertook.
- **Six objectives, numbered, each with its chapter:**
  1. **→ Chapter 1.** Analyse the clinical, epidemiological and technical context; characterise fundus image
     quality variability and device-specific variability; survey CNN architectures, transfer and in-domain
     pretraining, and explainability; critically analyse existing automated screening systems; formulate the
     research problem.
  2. **→ Chapter 2.** Establish the theoretical foundations: histogram equalisation and the dual-constraint
     CLAHE formalisation, spatial filtering, convolutional operations, losses for imbalanced data,
     regularisation, feature transferability and in-domain self-supervised pretraining, the coupled
     thermal-optical model of fundus tissue response (theoretical only — SB-1.5, SIR-6), CAM/Grad-CAM with
     ALO and IoU, and image-quality metrics.
  3. **→ Chapter 3.** Design and formalise the methodology: the eight-stage pipeline and its CLAHE and
     augmentation specifications, the external-image ingestion protocol, the architectures and their
     adaptation to five-class output, the initialisation and fine-tuning protocol, the loss formulation, and
     the multi-metric assessment and statistical-reliability framework.
  4. **→ Chapter 4.** Conduct the experimental programme — **eight investigations**: the 2×2 factorial
     contrast (H-1); the cumulative stage ablation with the CLAHE and flat-field parameter sweeps (H-2); the
     direct measurement of source-to-target distance in feature space across six external corpora (H-3);
     zero-shot transfer (H-4); attention–annotation alignment (H-5); external clinical performance (H-7);
     behaviour across camera groupings (H-6); and training in a data-scarce regime.
  5. **→ Chapter 5.** Validate reliability: interval estimation and cross-fold modelling, classification of
     each claim at the strength its pre-specified criterion supports, placement against published systems,
     the performance–complexity relation, and the limitations and boundary conditions.
  6. **→ Chapter 6.** Design a modular architecture for an automated screening system for resource-limited
     environments, as a design specification (SB-4.1).
- **Close (two sentences):** the mapping is exhaustive in both directions — every clause of the goal has an
  objective, and every objective answers to a clause — and the hypotheses that make objective 4 falsifiable
  are stated next.
