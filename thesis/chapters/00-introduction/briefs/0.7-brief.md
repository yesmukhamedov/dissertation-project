### CONTENT SPECIFICATION

**Section objective:** Give the methodological apparatus in four groups, so that a reader can see which
method serves which question, and close with the two concessions that make the apparatus honest.

**Argumentative spine:**
- **Opening.** The organising principle: a controlled experimental comparison in which everything outside
  the manipulated factor is held fixed — corpus, patient-level partitioning, training budget, hardware,
  evaluation protocol. This is the method; the rest are instruments serving it.
- **Group 1 — image processing.** Learned heatmap-regression landmark localisation for orientation
  normalisation; geometry-preserving isotropic resize; explicit field-of-view masking; adaptive flat-field
  illumination correction; dual-constraint CLAHE in the LAB colour space; dataset-specific normalisation.
- **Group 2 — learning.** Convolutional classification on two established backbones; transfer from
  natural-image pretraining for the baseline arm and in-domain initialisation *selected by a
  frozen-backbone acceptance gate* for the integrated arm; focal loss with inverse-frequency weighting for
  class imbalance.
- **Group 3 — evaluation and inference.** The multi-metric framework **in EH-1 order** — weighted F1 first,
  then ROC-AUC, then quadratic-weighted Cohen's κ, with accuracy reported but never sufficient alone;
  patient-level stratified five-fold cross-validation; paired significance testing; bootstrap interval
  estimation; a mixed-effects model across folds; and correction for multiplicity.
- **Group 4 — mechanism and interpretation.** Gradient-weighted class activation with Attention–Lesion
  Overlap primary and Intersection-over-Union secondary against pixel-level annotation; maximum mean
  discrepancy over penultimate-layer features with histogram divergence as informational context; and
  image-quality characterisation.
- **Close — the two concessions**, taken from §5.2.1 and stated here rather than only in Chapter 5:
  correction for multiplicity is **scoped to one experiment**, so no dissertation-wide error rate is
  claimed; and evaluations resting on a single fitted model quantify evaluation-corpus sampling alone and
  therefore **understate total uncertainty in a known direction**.
