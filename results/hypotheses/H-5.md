# H-5 — Explainability: Grad-CAM ALO/IoU (Exp 4) → PC-7

**Statement.** Grad-CAM for models with preprocessing yields **significantly** greater
Attention–Lesion Overlap (ALO, primary) and IoU (secondary) with the IDRiD pixel-level lesion masks
than baseline.

**Actual outcome (2026-08-03 run, n = 54 images with masks).** `h5_alo_supported = true`.

ALO is higher for the preprocessed model on **all 4 of 4** lesion types (the criterion is ≥3/4), and
on **all four** the difference is statistically significant:

| Type | n | ALO (baseline) → ALO (preproc) | Δ | 95% CI (Δ) | p (Wilcoxon, 1-sided) |
|---|--:|---|---:|---|---:|
| Microaneurysms | 54 | 0.2191 → 0.3208 | +0.1017 | [+0.0329, +0.1585] | 0.0029 |
| Haemorrhages | 53 | 0.2890 → 0.4022 | +0.1132 | [+0.0446, +0.1700] | 0.0017 |
| Hard exudates | 54 | 0.3528 → 0.4784 | +0.1256 | [+0.0729, +0.2001] | 0.0007 |
| Soft exudates | 26 | 0.2249 → 0.3381 | +0.1132 | [+0.0276, +0.1844] | 0.0147 |

The secondary metric IoU gives the same result on all four types (Δ +0.0608…+0.0918,
p = 0.0010…0.0195). Relative gains: ALO +36…50%, IoU +48…55%.

**Robustness to the binarization threshold.** The direction holds at **all** τ ∈ {0.2, 0.3, 0.5, 0.7}
(4/4); significance is lost for only one type at the strictest τ = 0.7 (3/4). The result is not an
artifact of choosing τ = 0.5.

**The floor effect has been eliminated.** ALO = 0 in both arms is observed for only **6 of 54**
images (f₀ = 0.1111). Measurement takes place in the working range of the metric, not at the edge of
sensitivity.

**The effect rests on individual observations, not on outliers.** Per image, 65–76% improve
(MA 38↑/7↓/9=, HE 36/8/9, EX 41/5/8, SE 17/4/5) and only 9–15% deteriorate. The mean shifts are the
result of a coherent movement of the majority.

**Consistency with classification.** The same two EfficientNet-B4 arms also differ in classification:
full wF1 0.7766 against baseline 0.7545 (+2.2 pp, +0.024 AUC, +0.047 κ). The gain appears
**simultaneously** along the discriminative and the localizational channel — the interpretation is
coherent: the pipeline both classifies better and aligns attention better.

**Mandatory boundary — INVARIANTS NC-14.** Grad-CAM activation **is not** clinical localization of
pathology. The correct formulation for §4.5/§5.1 is "the attention of the model with preprocessing is
significantly better aligned with the annotated lesions (4/4 types, p ≤ 0.0147; the same for IoU)",
and **not** "the model finds the lesions" or "the model is clinically interpretable". Confirmation of
H-5 does not weaken NC-14 — it measures alignment, not diagnostic localization.

**Caveats.**
- One model per arm (**fold 0**); there is no cross-validation for this analysis.
- Masks are available only for the IDRiD segmentation subset (54 images); soft exudates are annotated
  on 26 of them — hence the largest p is for SE.
- **The clinical (KZ) qualitative Grad-CAM overlays have not been produced** — the wording of H-5
  requires them. Gap **G-3**, the only one still open for this hypothesis (see `HYPOTHESIS_COVERAGE.md`).
