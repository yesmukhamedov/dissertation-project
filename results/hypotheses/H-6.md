# H-6 — Device / Camera Domain-Shift Resistance (Exp 6)

**Statement.** The full pipeline preserves performance under a change of device/camera —
g_ratio ≥ 0.7 across the camera groups (IDRiD, DDR, ODIR-5K, Messidor-2, RFMiD).

**Actual outcome (2026-08-02 run).** `h6_supported = true`. All 5 groups are above the 0.7 floor for
both arms; the pipeline's minimum g_ratio is 0.7909 (mixed_rfmid), a margin of 0.09 over the threshold.

| Group | g_ratio (C) | g_ratio (D) | Δ wF1 (D − C) | 95% CI (Δ) |
|--------|------------:|------------:|--------------:|------------|
| kowa_idrid | 0.7854 | 0.8080 | +0.0700 | [+0.0463, +0.0937] |
| mixed_ddr | 0.8145 | 0.8190 | +0.0570 | [+0.0338, +0.0802] |
| mixed_odir5k | 0.7535 | 0.8007 | +0.0880 | [+0.0621, +0.1139] |
| topcon_messidor2 | 0.8318 | 0.8275 | +0.0510 | [+0.0284, +0.0736] |
| mixed_rfmid | 0.7310 | 0.7909 | +0.0970 | [+0.0698, +0.1242] |

**Honest interpretation — the main result is not the threshold but the spread.** The threshold is
cleared by **both** arms, so the mere fact of "g ≥ 0.7" does not discriminate between them. The
substantive result is the **significant reduction in between-camera spread**:

| Quantity | C | D | Δ | 95% CI (Δ) |
|----------|--:|--:|--:|------------|
| std (wF1, 5 groups) | 0.0281 | 0.0106 | −0.0175 | [−0.0268, −0.0082] |
| std (ROC-AUC, 5 groups) | 0.0210 | 0.0068 | −0.0142 | [−0.0221, −0.0063] |

The wF1 spread falls by **a factor of 2.6** and the AUC spread by **a factor of 3.1**, with both CIs
excluding zero. The g_ratio range contracts from 0.7310–0.8318 (span 0.101) to 0.7909–0.8275
(span 0.037).

**The levelling mechanism.** The gain is larger the worse a group performed under baseline: the
largest Δ wF1 are for mixed_rfmid (+0.0970, C's worst group) and mixed_odir5k (+0.0880), and the
smallest for topcon_messidor2 (+0.0510, C's best group). The pipeline raises the floor more than it
raises the ceiling — and that is what produces the contraction of the spread. This is consistent with
[[H-3]]: the largest MMD-distance reductions are likewise for RFMiD (+0.0880) and ODIR-5K (+0.0850).

**The only exception.** For topcon_messidor2 the pipeline's g_ratio is slightly lower (0.8275 against
0.8318). Its absolute wF1 is nevertheless higher (0.6780 against 0.6270) — the drop in g_ratio is
entirely explained by the larger denominator (in-domain 0.8193 against 0.7538), not by worse
performance. Flag it as a normalization artifact, not as a counterexample.

**Clinical part.** The pipeline improves **all four** clinical quantities in **all five** groups,
without exception. The sensitivity range narrows from 0.654–0.748 (span 0.094) to 0.791–0.836
(span 0.045) — the operating point becomes both better and more stable across devices.
Referable AUC 0.853–0.908 → 0.914–0.942.

**Caveats.**
- Evaluation uses **fold 0** checkpoints; the std in the spread table is **between-group** (over the
  5 groups), not between-fold.
- The `mixed_rfmid` group was evaluated on the 5-class scale in this run (in the previous run it was
  binary only) — it is not comparable with the earlier numbers.
- The groups overlap with the exp5 sets: `kowa_idrid` = IDRiD, `topcon_messidor2` = Messidor-2; the
  values coincide ([[H-7]]).

**For the thesis.** Formulation: "the pipeline preserves performance above the generalization floor
on every camera group and **significantly reduces the between-camera spread** (std wF1 by 2.6×, std
AUC by 3.1×), lifting the most problematic devices above all". Practical implication for deployment:
device-specific threshold recalibration ceases to be mandatory. Links: [[H-3]], [[H-4]], [[H-1]].
