# H-6 — Device / Camera Domain-Shift Resistance (Exp 6)

**Statement.** The full pipeline preserves performance under a change of device/camera —
g_ratio ≥ 0.7 across the camera groups (IDRiD, DDR, ODIR-5K, Messidor-2, RFMiD).

**Actual outcome (2026-08-03 run).** `h6_supported = true`. All 5 groups are above the 0.7 floor for
both arms; the pipeline's minimum g_ratio is 0.7837 (mixed_rfmid), a margin of 0.084 over the threshold.

| Group | g_ratio (C) | g_ratio (D) | Δ wF1 (D − C) | 95% CI (Δ) |
|--------|------------:|------------:|--------------:|------------|
| kowa_idrid | 0.7903 | 0.8046 | +0.0635 | [+0.0445, +0.0919] |
| mixed_ddr | 0.8164 | 0.8142 | +0.0517 | [+0.0226, +0.0690] |
| mixed_odir5k | 0.7562 | 0.8032 | +0.0881 | [+0.0570, +0.1088] |
| topcon_messidor2 | 0.8335 | 0.8311 | +0.0526 | [+0.0264, +0.0716] |
| mixed_rfmid | 0.7209 | 0.7837 | +0.0987 | [+0.0680, +0.1224] |

**Honest interpretation — the main result is not the threshold but the spread.** The threshold is
cleared by **both** arms, so the mere fact of "g ≥ 0.7" does not discriminate between them. The
substantive result is the **significant reduction in between-camera spread**:

| Quantity | C | D | Δ | 95% CI (Δ) |
|----------|--:|--:|--:|------------|
| std (wF1, 5 groups) | 0.0307 | 0.0127 | −0.0180 | [−0.0253, −0.0062] |
| std (ROC-AUC, 5 groups) | 0.0214 | 0.0070 | −0.0144 | [−0.0233, −0.0072] |

The wF1 spread falls by **a factor of 2.4** and the AUC spread by **a factor of 3.1**, with both CIs
excluding zero. The g_ratio range contracts from 0.7209–0.8335 (span 0.113) to 0.7837–0.8311
(span 0.047).

**The levelling mechanism.** The gain is larger the worse a group performed under baseline: the
largest Δ wF1 are for mixed_rfmid (+0.0987, C's worst group) and mixed_odir5k (+0.0881), and the
smallest for mixed_ddr (+0.0517) and topcon_messidor2 (+0.0526, C's best group). The pipeline raises
the floor more than it raises the ceiling — and that is what produces the contraction of the spread.
Consistent with [[H-3]] at the extreme: RFMiD has both the largest MMD reduction (+0.0931) and the
largest gain; across the middle of the range the two orderings do not track each other.

**g_ratio falls in 2 of 5 groups — a normalization artifact, not a counterexample.** mixed_ddr
(0.8164 → 0.8142) and topcon_messidor2 (0.8335 → 0.8311) are exactly the two groups with the smallest
absolute gains, and both were already strong under baseline. Absolute wF1 rises in both
(0.6154 → 0.6671 and 0.6283 → 0.6809). g_ratio divides by each arm's **own** in-domain wF1, and the
pipeline's denominator is 6.55 pp larger, so a group has to gain roughly 8% relative merely to hold
its ratio. This must be stated in the text: **g_ratio systematically understates the pipeline's
advantage**, and the H-6 verdict rests on the floor criterion (5/5 above 0.7 for both arms) plus the
spread reduction, not on the ratio rising everywhere.

**Clinical part.** The pipeline improves **all four** clinical quantities in **all five** groups,
without exception. The sensitivity range narrows from 0.651–0.748 (span 0.096) to 0.794–0.835
(span 0.041) — the operating point becomes both better and more stable across devices.
Referable AUC 0.855–0.906 → 0.930–0.946.

**Caveats.**
- Evaluation uses **fold 0** checkpoints; the std in the spread table is **between-group** (over the
  5 groups), not between-fold.
- The `mixed_rfmid` group was evaluated on the 5-class scale in this run (in the previous run it was
  binary only) — it is not comparable with the earlier numbers.
- The groups overlap with the exp5 sets: `kowa_idrid` = IDRiD, `topcon_messidor2` = Messidor-2; the
  values coincide ([[H-7]]).

**For the thesis.** Formulation: "the pipeline preserves performance above the generalization floor
on every camera group and **significantly reduces the between-camera spread** (std wF1 by 2.4×, std
AUC by 3.1×), lifting the most problematic devices above all". Practical implication for deployment:
device-specific threshold recalibration ceases to be mandatory. Links: [[H-3]], [[H-4]], [[H-1]].
