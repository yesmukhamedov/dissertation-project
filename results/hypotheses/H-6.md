# H-6 — Device / Camera Domain-Shift Resistance (Exp 6)

**Statement.** The full pipeline preserves performance under a change of device/camera —
g_ratio ≥ 0.7 across the camera groups (IDRiD, DDR, ODIR-5K, Messidor-2, RFMiD).

**Actual outcome (2026-08-03 run).** `h6_supported = true`. All 5 groups are above the 0.7 floor for
both arms; the pipeline's minimum g_ratio is 0.7863 (mixed_rfmid), a margin of 0.086 over the threshold.

| Group | g_ratio (C) | g_ratio (D) | Δ wF1 (D − C) | 95% CI (Δ) |
|--------|------------:|------------:|--------------:|------------|
| kowa_idrid | 0.7844 | 0.8072 | +0.0700 | [+0.0526, +0.1000] |
| mixed_ddr | 0.8107 | 0.8169 | +0.0582 | [+0.0296, +0.0760] |
| mixed_odir5k | 0.7600 | 0.8013 | +0.0836 | [+0.0522, +0.1040] |
| topcon_messidor2 | 0.8331 | 0.8349 | +0.0560 | [+0.0355, +0.0807] |
| mixed_rfmid | 0.7355 | 0.7863 | +0.0898 | [+0.0659, +0.1203] |

**Honest interpretation — the main result is not the threshold but the spread.** The threshold is
cleared by **both** arms, so the mere fact of "g ≥ 0.7" does not discriminate between them. The
substantive result is the **significant reduction in between-camera spread**:

| Quantity | C | D | Δ | 95% CI (Δ) |
|----------|--:|--:|--:|------------|
| std (wF1, 5 groups) | 0.0262 | 0.0133 | −0.0129 | [−0.0186, −0.0049] |
| std (ROC-AUC, 5 groups) | 0.0209 | 0.0064 | −0.0145 | [−0.0247, −0.0085] |

The wF1 spread falls by **a factor of 2.0** and the AUC spread by **a factor of 3.3**, with both CIs
excluding zero. The g_ratio range contracts from 0.7355–0.8331 (span 0.098) to 0.7863–0.8349
(span 0.049).

**The levelling mechanism.** The gain is larger the worse a group performed under baseline: the
largest Δ wF1 are for mixed_rfmid (+0.0898, C's worst group) and mixed_odir5k (+0.0836), and the
smallest for topcon_messidor2 (+0.0560, C's best group). The pipeline raises the floor more than it
raises the ceiling — and that is what produces the contraction of the spread. This is consistent with
[[H-3]]: the largest MMD-distance reductions are likewise for ODIR-5K (+0.0937) and RFMiD (+0.0864).

**No exceptions in this run.** g_ratio rises in **all five** groups, including topcon_messidor2
(0.8331 → 0.8349), where the previous run showed an inversion. On that group the g_ratio gain is
marginal (+0.0018) against a clear absolute gain (wF1 0.6280 → 0.6840) — the ratio is damped by the
pipeline's larger denominator (in-domain 0.8193 against 0.7538). Flag that g_ratio understates the
advantage by construction.

**Clinical part.** The pipeline improves **all four** clinical quantities in **all five** groups,
without exception. The sensitivity range narrows from 0.655–0.750 (span 0.096) to 0.793–0.834
(span 0.041) — the operating point becomes both better and more stable across devices.
Referable AUC 0.851–0.910 → 0.910–0.946.

**Caveats.**
- Evaluation uses **fold 0** checkpoints; the std in the spread table is **between-group** (over the
  5 groups), not between-fold.
- The `mixed_rfmid` group was evaluated on the 5-class scale in this run (in the previous run it was
  binary only) — it is not comparable with the earlier numbers.
- The groups overlap with the exp5 sets: `kowa_idrid` = IDRiD, `topcon_messidor2` = Messidor-2; the
  values coincide ([[H-7]]).

**For the thesis.** Formulation: "the pipeline preserves performance above the generalization floor
on every camera group and **significantly reduces the between-camera spread** (std wF1 by 2.0×, std
AUC by 3.3×), lifting the most problematic devices above all". Practical implication for deployment:
device-specific threshold recalibration ceases to be mandatory. Links: [[H-3]], [[H-4]], [[H-1]].
