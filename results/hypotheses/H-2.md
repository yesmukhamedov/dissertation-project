# H-2 — Component Ablation + CLAHE/σ Sensitivity (Exp 2) → PC-2, PC-8

**Statement of H-2 (HYPOTHESIS.md).** Varying the parameters of dual-constraint CLAHE (and the
flat-field σ) on EyePACS produces a parameter-dependent per-class F1 profile (especially for DR1/DR2)
with ≥1 local optimum inside the tested range; the component ablation reveals the contribution of the
stages.

**Actual protocol (2026-08-03 run):** the cumulative ablation — **EyePACS 100% (n = 35 126),
5 folds, EfficientNet-B3**, 8 levels; the CLAHE sweep — a 7 × 5 two-dimensional grid
(clip_factor × global_threshold) on EyePACS; the flat-field σ sweep — 6 values over 0.05–0.10·D.
All three parts were run.

> Relative to the previous version, gaps **G-5** (the σ sweep had not been implemented), **G-6**
> (only clip_factor was varied), **G-7** (the sweep ran on IDRiD rather than EyePACS) and largely
> **G-8** (Stage 1 and Stages 2–3 are now isolated by separate levels) are closed. The protocol is no
> longer "15% / 3 folds / ImageNet init" — the numbers are directly comparable with exp1.

## PC-2 (CLAHE/σ sensitivity) — FULLY CONFIRMED

**CLAHE.** The two-dimensional profile is non-monotone in both parameters, with an **interior**
maximum: θ\* = (clip_factor 2.5, global_threshold 0.03), p_apply = 0.80. Per-class optima exist and
**do not coincide across classes**: θ̂(DR1) = (2.5, 0.03), θ̂(DR2) = (2.0, 0.03) — the finer DR1
features require a more aggressive clip. Held-out: wF1 0.7538 → 0.8137 (Δ +0.0599,
CI [+0.0388, +0.0770]), F1(DR1) 0.0976 → 0.2091, F1(DR2) 0.5316 → 0.6477.

**Flat-field σ.** The profile is strictly unimodal with an interior maximum at σ\* = 0.07·D; the
range across the sweep, R = 0.0512, is comparable to the full pipeline effect, i.e. the sensitivity
is high. Held-out: 0.7513 → 0.8087 (Δ +0.0574, CI [+0.0428, +0.0806]). σ\* coincides with the value
fixed in the Stage 4 specification — the sweep confirms the adopted setting.

## PC-8 (hierarchy of stage contributions) — ALL SEVEN SIGNIFICANT, HIERARCHY RESOLVABLE

The cumulative ablation L0 → L7: wF1 0.7538 → 0.8193 (+0.0655), monotone, **without a single
inversion in any of the 5 folds**. All seven transitions are significant: |Δⱼ| = 0.0065–0.0143
against 2·σ_fold = 0.0042–0.0060.

> ⚠️ **This reverses the previous revision.** The earlier run gave Δⱼ = 0.0090–0.0100 (spread 0.0010
> < σ_fold) and PC-8 was reported as "contributions significant, hierarchy flat, stages not
> rankable". In the current run the spread is **0.0078 ≈ 3·σ_fold**, so the hierarchy **is**
> resolvable. Formulations built on "the pipeline is an ensemble of equals" must be replaced.

The ranking (Δⱼ, share of the +0.0655 total):

| Rank | Stage | Δⱼ | share |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | Stage 4 — flat-field | **0.0143** | 22% |
| 2 | Stage 5 — CLAHE | **0.0125** | 19% |
| 3 | Stage 6 — augmentation | 0.0101 | 15% |
| 4 | Stages 2–3 — FOV crop + mask | 0.0082 | 13% |
| 5 | Stage 0 — canonical flip | 0.0071 | 11% |
| 6 | Stage 1 — OD-fovea rotation | 0.0068 | 10% |
| 7 | Stage 7 — normalize → tensor | 0.0065 | 10% |

The substantive reading: the **photometric** pair (flat-field + CLAHE) carries **41%** of the total
gain — as much as the four geometric/normalization stages together. This aligns the ablation with the
sweeps (both photometric parameters show sharp interior optima, PC-2) and with [[H-3]] (the pipeline
reduces domain distance mainly through illumination and contrast). No stage is redundant: the weakest
still clears its own 2·σ_fold threshold.

Limits of the formulation:

- **what is claimed**: every stage contributes significantly, and the two photometric stages
  contribute markedly more than the rest;
- **what is not claimed**: a strict 1-to-7 ordering — adjacent ranks (Stage 1 at 0.0068 vs Stage 7 at
  0.0065) sit within noise. The data resolve the **grouping**, not the full permutation.

## Key consequence: decomposition of the CFC-2.8 confound

The endpoints of the ablation reproduce exp1 exactly: L0 = 0.7538 = Config C, L7 = 0.8193 = Config D —
under **a single initialization at all eight levels**. Hence the entire D-vs-C gain (+0.0655) belongs
to preprocessing, not to the SSL initialization. The former conclusion that "the exp1 effect is an
indivisible preprocessing + SSL composite" (and the argument resting on it, that preprocessing on its
own delivers nothing) is **refuted by this run**. Links: [[H-1]], [[exp7-and-SSL]].

## Image quality ≠ classification quality — the thesis holds, in a sharpened form

The full pipeline improves both IQ (CNR +18%, Entropy +0.40 bits) and classification. The new
ablation numbers make the relationship more legible than before: **the only two levels that move the
IQ metrics are the two top-ranked contributors** — L4 (flat-field) jumps CNR 20.38 → 28.60 and is
rank 1 at Δⱼ = 0.0143; L5 (CLAHE) jumps Entropy 5.596 → 5.884 and is rank 2 at 0.0125. Where the IQ
metrics see something, they see the largest effects.

But they remain **insufficient**, because they are blind to the rest: the geometric levels L1–L3
deliver +2.21 pp wF1 with CNR/Entropy unchanged, and L6 (augmentation) delivers +1.01 pp with all
three metrics completely unchanged — together 49% of the total gain, invisible to IQ. And the CNR
maximum still falls at L4 while wF1 keeps rising through L7. Within a single stage (the flat-field σ
sweep) CNR and wF1 move together perfectly. The correct formulation: **the IQ metrics track the
photometric part of the mechanism — which is the largest single part — but do not exhaust it**, and
they are not a sufficient predictor of the classification gain.

## Caveats

- The order in which stages are added is fixed (the pipeline order) → Δⱼ is the contribution of a
  stage **given that the preceding ones are already applied**; interactions between stages are not
  measured.
- **Stage 3 (FOV mask) is not isolated**: level L3 adds Stage 2 and Stage 3 jointly (disabling the
  mask requires a 3-channel model variant). This is the remainder of gap G-8 — and it now matters more
  than before, since the stages are being ranked: rank 4 belongs to the *pair*, not to either stage.
- The sweep grids were obtained on train folds, one evaluation per point, without std.
- The held-out F1(DR1) at θ\* (0.2091) is substantially below the grid value (0.4693); the
  discrepancy is not explained by the available data — **use the held-out value in the text**.
- The CLAHE and σ sweeps were run independently; no joint three-parameter grid was built.
