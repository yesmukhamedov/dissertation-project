# H-7 — External Clinical Performance (Exp 5)

**Statement (operative form).** On external clinical datasets — different hardware, different
population — the integrated configuration delivers higher **absolute** weighted-F1 than baseline,
zero-shot and without retraining. The comparison is arm-wise: the pair **D − C** (backbone E3).

**Criterion.** N = 2, both sets mandatory, form S on each:

```
H-7  ⟺  ⋀            PASS_S(wF1, D−C on X) = 1
        X ∈ {IDRiD, Messidor-2}

PASS_S  ⟺  Δ wF1(X) ≥ MCID_wF1 = 0.050   ∧   CI⁻ > 0
```

The sets are **not aggregated** — a single REV would give REVERSED independently of the other.

**Actual outcome (2026-08-03 run).** `h7_supported = true` — **CONFIRMED (2/2)**.

| Set | n | wF1 (C) | wF1 (D) | Δ | 95% CI (Δ) | p (1-sided) |
|-------|--:|--------:|--------:|--:|------------|------------:|
| IDRiD | 413 | 0.5938 | 0.6627 | +0.0689 | [+0.0494, +0.0968] | 0.0021 |
| Messidor-2 | 1 744 | 0.6282 | 0.6823 | +0.0541 | [+0.0362, +0.0814] | 0.0138 |

| Condition | IDRiD | Messidor-2 |
|---|---|---|
| Δ ≥ 0.050 | 0.0689 ✓ | 0.0541 ✓ |
| CI⁻ > 0 | +0.0494 ✓ | +0.0362 ✓ |
| **PASS_S** | **1** | **1** |
| REV_S | 0 | 0 |

> **Form S requires Δ ≥ MCID and CI⁻ > 0 — not CI⁻ ≥ MCID.** On Messidor-2 the lower bound (+0.0362)
> lies below the 0.050 threshold; that does not block the pass. The margin on Δ itself is thin
> there — **0.0041** — and should be stated rather than glossed.

## Honest interpretation

The claim is narrow and it holds cleanly: transferred to two external clinics with no adaptation, the
pipeline is better by a clinically meaningful margin on both. That is the quantity that determines
usability on deployment at a new site.

What is **not** claimed is a resistance effect. The proportional drop from in-domain is essentially
the same for the two arms (21.2% vs 19.1% on IDRiD, 16.7% vs 16.7% on Messidor-2). The pipeline does
not degrade less — it starts higher and finishes higher.

## Δ_drop — retired, kept for reference

| Set | Δ_drop (C) | Δ_drop (D) | difference | relative |
|-------|-----------:|-----------:|-----------:|---|
| IDRiD | 0.1600 | **0.1566** | −0.0034 | 21.2% vs **19.1%** |
| Messidor-2 | **0.1256** | 0.1370 | +0.0114 | 16.7% vs 16.7% |

The original form of H-7 — "the Δ_drop of the integrated arm is statistically smaller" — was retired
because the quantity is not independent of the hypothesis it was meant to test:

```
Δ_drop(D,X) − Δ_drop(C,X) = Δ_in-domain − Δ_external = 0.0655 − Δ wF1(X)
```

Exact on both sets: IDRiD 0.0655 − 0.0689 = −0.0034; Messidor-2 0.0655 − 0.0541 = +0.0114.

The sign is therefore decided solely by whether the external margin exceeds the fixed in-domain gap
of 0.0655. The criterion demands that the pipeline beat baseline **more on foreign data than at
home**, and penalizes the integrated arm for its own in-domain result. It measures nothing about
resistance, which is why it was retired. **This identity is a contribution for §5.4 in its own
right** — a critique of a degradation metric in common use in the domain-shift literature.

By that reference metric the split is 1/2 (IDRiD in the pipeline's favour, Messidor-2 in baseline's).
It must be reported as descriptive only, never as the verdict.

## Caveats

- Evaluation uses **fold 0** checkpoints; there is no between-fold variance.
- The Messidor-2 margin over MCID is 0.0041 — a re-run moving Δ by more than that flips the set.
- CIs are asymmetric percentile bootstrap.
- The same two sets appear in exp6 as camera groups (`kowa_idrid`, `topcon_messidor2`) — values, Δ and
  CI coincide character-for-character by construction ([[H-6]]).

## For the thesis

Report as **confirmed (2/2)**: "on transfer to external clinical sets without retraining, the
integrated configuration delivers absolute weighted-F1 higher than baseline by +0.069 (IDRiD) and
+0.054 (Messidor-2), both above the MCID of 0.05 and with intervals excluding zero".

State separately, in §5.4, the methodological observation: **Δ_drop is not a neutral measure of
resistance when the in-domain levels are unequal** — it reduces algebraically to the in-domain gap
minus the external gap, so it penalizes the stronger arm by construction. That analysis is why the
hypothesis carries the present wording.

Contrast with [[exp7-and-SSL]]: when training **within** the target clinical domain the pipeline
delivers +0.080 wF1.
