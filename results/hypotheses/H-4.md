# H-4 — Cross-Dataset Transferability to APTOS (Exp 3)

**Statement.** The full pipeline achieves generalization to APTOS 2019 with the ratio
**G = F1_APTOS / F1_EyePACS ≥ 0.85** (better than baseline).

**Actual outcome (2026-08-03 run).** `h4_supported = true`.
- Full pipeline (Config D): G = **0.8966** (APTOS wF1 0.7346) — the threshold is cleared with a
  margin of 0.047.
- Baseline (Config C): G = **0.8569** (APTOS wF1 0.6459).
- Δ wF1 (D − C) = **+0.0887**, 95% CI [+0.0572, +0.1088]; Δ AUC = +0.0368, CI [+0.0211, +0.0469].

**Honest interpretation.** Both parts of the hypothesis are met, but with a substantial caveat: **the
G ≥ 0.85 threshold is cleared by baseline as well** (0.8569). That is, the "G ≥ 0.85" criterion on
its own does not discriminate between the arms — what discriminates them is the second part, "better
than baseline", and that is met convincingly (the Δ CIs exclude zero on both primary metrics). It
should be phrased as "the pipeline does not rescue transfer, it improves transfer that is already
acceptable", without claiming that baseline transfers poorly.

**Mechanism.** The pipeline holds on to the **intermediate grades**, where transfer usually collapses:
F1(DR2) 0.5736 → 0.6920, F1(DR1) 0.1375 → 0.2710. In the confusion matrix the mass of DR2 → DR1 falls
from 245 to 193 instances and DR2 → DR0 from 96 to 35. Errors remain adjacent on the scale (the
DR0 → DR4 cell: 2 → 0), which is what explains the large κ gain (0.7865 → 0.8834). There is a gain on
**all five** grades; macro-F1 rises more than weighted-F1 (+0.102 against +0.089).

**Clinical part.** Binary "refer / do not refer" triage transfers for both arms (referable AUC 0.890
and 0.934), but the pipeline gives a clinically more favourable operating point: Sens 0.7330 → 0.8346
(+10.2 pp) **with specificity rising at the same time** (0.9200 → 0.9411). The same sensitivity gain
(+0.10…+0.11) as in-domain and across the camera groups.

**Relation to the mechanism.** The EyePACS↔APTOS MMD distance is reduced by the pipeline from 0.1886
to 0.1139 (Δd +0.0747, CI excludes zero) — see [[H-3]]. The transfer gain is consistent with the
reduction in domain distance.

**Caveats.**
- Evaluation uses **fold 0** checkpoints; there is no between-fold variance for this experiment, and
  the 95% CIs are per-instance (bootstrap).
- G is normalized by each arm's **own** in-domain wF1 (C: 0.7538, D: 0.8193). The arm with the higher
  in-domain score has the larger denominator, so the gain in G (+0.040) is by construction more
  conservative than the gain in absolute APTOS wF1 (+0.089). Worth stating so that the reader does
  not read the modest ΔG as a weak effect.

**For the thesis.** Report as a confirmed hypothesis with two caveats (both arms clear the threshold;
fold 0). Links: [[H-3]] (mechanism), [[H-6]] (the same pattern across camera groups), [[H-7]].
