# H-1 — Integrated Pipeline Dominance (Exp 1)

**Statement.** The full 8-stage pipeline (4ch) dominates baseline (3ch) on EyePACS under the EH-3
criterion (ΔF1 ≥ 5 pp AND ΔAUC ≥ 0.02 AND no degradation in κ), on both backbones.

**Actual outcome (2026-08-03 run).** `h1_supported = true`.
- B vs A (ResNet-50): ΔF1 **+6.54 pp**, ΔAUC **+0.0320**, Δκ **+0.1129** → dominance YES.
- D vs C (EffNet-B3): ΔF1 **+6.55 pp**, ΔAUC **+0.0360**, Δκ **+0.1103** → dominance YES.

All six 95% CIs on the differences exclude zero. Significance: DeLong p = 0.0041 / 0.0028,
McNemar p = 0.0057 / 0.0041, and after the Holm correction over 4 configurations — 0.0082 / 0.0056.
Mixed-effects ANOVA: the "arm × backbone" interaction p = 0.31 → **the effect is statistically the
same on both architectures**, which is exactly what the phrase "on both backbones" requires.

**Honest interpretation.** The criterion is not met by a hair's breadth: the F1 margin is +1.5 pp
above the 5 pp threshold, the AUC margin +0.012…+0.016 above the 0.02 threshold, and κ rises by +0.11
where the requirement was merely "does not degrade". The effect size for ResNet-50 and
EfficientNet-B3 agrees to the second decimal (6.54 vs 6.55 pp), which is consistent with the absence
of an interaction and indicates that the effect belongs to the pipeline rather than to a fortunate
pairing with one particular architecture.

The mechanism is legible from the secondary metrics: macro-F1 rises more than weighted-F1 (+0.104
against +0.065) — the gain is disproportionately concentrated on the rare classes; F1(DR1) doubles
(0.0999 → 0.2141). Distant grading errors almost disappear (the DR0 → DR4 cell: 26 → 4 instances),
which is what explains the large quadratic-κ gain. Clinically: sensitivity to referable DR rises by
+11.2 pp **with specificity rising at the same time**.

**Accompanying results that used to be drawbacks:**
- **Calibration now improves** rather than degrades: ECE 0.0712 → 0.0418 and 0.0691 → 0.0402
  (`tables/TAB-4.3_exp1_calibration.md`). The former mandatory caveat that "the pipeline is
  overconfident and needs recalibration" has been withdrawn.
- **The regularizing effect persists:** loss gap 0.052 → 0.021 and 0.054 → 0.022, convergence 6–7
  epochs earlier, with a **higher** train_loss in the pipeline arms.

**Caveat (CFC-2.8) — its form has changed.** The integrated arm is still initialized with
continual-SSL, so Config B/D formally measure the composite "preprocessing × initialization". But the
composite is now **decomposable**: the cumulative ablation on the same corpus and split under **a
single initialization at all eight levels** yields ΔwF1 = +0.0655 from L0 to L7 — exactly the D-vs-C
magnitude (`tables/TAB-4.4_exp2_ablation.md`, where the ablation endpoints coincide with Config C and
D). That is, the contribution of preprocessing has been measured separately, and the claim "the gain
comes from preprocessing" rests on direct measurement rather than attribution. CFC-2.8 goes into the
text as a note about the design, not as a limitation on the conclusion.

**For the thesis.** Formulation of the contribution: "the integrated configuration with the 8-stage
pipeline dominates baseline on all three components of the EH-3 criterion on both backbones
(ΔwF1 +6.5 pp, ΔAUC +0.032…0.036, Δκ +0.11); the effect is significant after correction for
multiplicity and does not depend on the choice of architecture; the contribution of preprocessing is
independently confirmed by the cumulative ablation at fixed initialization". Links: [[H-2]]
(decomposition by stage), [[H-3]] (the mechanism — reduction of domain distance), [[H-6]],
[[exp7-and-SSL]].
