# Research aim and objectives

## На слайде

**Aim** — to develop and experimentally validate an integrated model for automated five-class diagnosis of diabetic retinopathy in which an eight-stage preprocessing pipeline is a component of the model, and to determine what this gives in accuracy, transferability and interpretability under limited computing resources.

**Objectives**
1. Analyse the problem domain and formulate the research problem (Ch. 1)
2. Define the integrated methodology: pipeline, classification architectures, pretraining, interpretability, evaluation protocol (Ch. 2)
3. Evaluate the model in-domain and on seven external corpora and validate the results statistically (Ch. 3)
4. Build a screening system for settings without an accelerator and with intermittent connectivity (Ch. 4)

## Речь

The aim is to develop an integrated model in which the eight-stage preprocessing pipeline is a model component, and to establish experimentally what it contributes by comparison with an equivalent configuration without the pipeline. The aim is reached through four objectives, one per chapter: analysis, methodology, experimental evaluation and the screening system.
