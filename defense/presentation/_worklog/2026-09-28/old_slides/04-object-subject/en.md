# Object, subject and methods of research

## На слайде

**Object** — the process of automated multi-stage diagnosis of diabetic retinopathy from colour fundus photographs with convolutional networks: from the image leaving the camera to the grade the model returns.

**Subject** — methods for integrating preprocessing with classification and the properties of the resulting configuration: diagnostic performance, transferability, alignment of attention with pathology, behaviour across cameras.

**Methods** — controlled 2×2 factorial experiment; cumulative ablation; patient-level stratified 5-fold cross-validation; DeLong and McNemar tests, Holm correction, bootstrap, mixed-effects model; Grad-CAM; MMD / KL domain distance.

## Речь

The object is the process of automated retinopathy diagnosis from colour fundus images, as a whole, starting at the camera. The subject is the methods for integrating preprocessing with classification and the properties of the configuration. The main method is controlled comparison: everything but the studied factor is fixed.
